Encyclopedia, not instruction

Martial Index is a written encyclopedia and catalog of combat sports and martial arts. Entries describe positions, techniques, history, and related concepts for reference. Reading a page is not a substitute for in-person coaching, supervised drilling, or live sparring under qualified instruction.

Train with qualified coaches

Combat sports involve risk of injury. Learn and practice under qualified coaches in an appropriate facility. Do not attempt unfamiliar submissions, throws, strikes, or weapons material from text alone. Respect your partners and the rules of your gym or sport.

Tap early

When a choke, joint lock, or crushing pressure is locked in, tap early. Pride is not a good reason to wait. If you are coaching or rolling with someone less experienced, prioritize their safety over finishing for the highlight.

Accuracy and changes

We aim for clear, careful writing, but martial arts terminology varies by gym, lineage, and rule set. Entries may be incomplete, revised, or corrected over time. Martial Index does not guarantee that any description is exhaustive or competition-legal in every organization.
