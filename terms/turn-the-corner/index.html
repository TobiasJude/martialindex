Turn the Corner

Turn the corner means running your hips past theirs on a Single-Leg Takedown or high crotch—especially when a Sprawl or the fence blocks a straight dump—so you finish behind or to the side.

By Tobias Mesevage · Published 12 Sep 2026 · Updated 12 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Do not stay stuck in front of the hips.
  • Circle toward the trapped-leg side.
  • Fence can help or hinder—read it.
  • Chain to re-shoot if the leg frees.
  • Cross-link {single-leg-takedown}.

How Turn the Corner Works

On a single/high crotch → keep the leg → circle toward the trapped leg side → get your hips past theirs → finish the dump or mat return.

Failing to turn the corner leaves you in the 'headlight' in front of their hips—prime for crossfaces, whizzers, and front headlocks against you.

Mechanically, successful turn the corner work is a chain: establish grip or angle → remove a base or frame → apply the scoring action → recover posture with hands alive.

If distance or fence angle is wrong, even perfect form fails—too far and you claw air; too close without a pin and you collide into frames, whizzers, or submission threats.

Chain to Re-Shoot if you lose the leg during the turn; chain to High Crotch to Cage if the wall arrives mid-turn.

Common Mistakes

  • Turning the wrong direction into their free hip.
  • Loosing the leg mid-circle.
  • Posture so high you get guillotined.
  • Stopping the circle halfway.
  • Treating turn the corner as a highlight instead of a position-and-timing skill.
  • Skipping recovery—landing then posing with hands down or hips light.
  • Ignoring sibling tools that set or cover the technique.

Study Notes

Coaches often describe turn the corner as “simple to show, hard to own.” The gap is usually not a missing secret—it is missing reps under stress, poor fence awareness, or grips that collapse when strikes enter the round.

When you compare gyms, you will see stylistic accents: some put turn the corner behind heavy wrestling; others behind dirty boxing; submission-heavy rooms may leave GNP light. The encyclopedia definition stays stable even when accents change.
