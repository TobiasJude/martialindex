Spats

Spats are tight compression leggings used in no-gi grappling for coverage, hygiene, and friction management—often paired with shorts.

By Tobias Mesevage · Published 16 Sep 2026 · Updated 16 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Spats = tight leggings designed for grappling movement.
  • Common in no-gi; some wear them under gi pants for skin protection.
  • Check event attire rules—federations differ on logos/coverage.
  • Pair with legal no-gi shorts.
  • Hygiene and mat burn reduction are practical benefits—not fashion-only.

What Are Spats?

Spats are stretch compression pants worn for grappling. They reduce skin-on-mat scrapes and can limit some skin-to-skin friction in no-gi rooms.

How It Works

Athletes pull spats on like athletic leggings, often add shorts over them for pocketless coverage and academy dress codes, then train.

Gi vs No-Gi

Primarily a no-gi garment. Under-gi use exists for rash protection but is optional and gym-dependent.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

At no-gi open mat you wear spats + shorts + rashguard, avoid zippers, and stay within house dress code.

Types / Variations

Common patterns

Full-length spats; capri lengths; grappling-specific vs generic compression tights.

Gym / sport variants

Some gyms require shorts over spats. Competition rules vary—verify event packets and compare IBJJF/ADCC attire notes at a high level.

Beginner notes

Borrow or buy simple black spats; prioritize durability and coverage over loud designs if competing soon.

How to Apply / Practical Tips

Wash after every session. Avoid cotton sweatpants with pockets. Match shorts length to gym rules.

How It Compares

Different from gi pants. Related to rashguards as compression layers for the upper body.

Special Considerations

Rules & sport context

Attire legality is event-specific—verify current guides rather than assuming. See also IBJJF Rules / ADCC Rules attire sections.

Safety

No metal. Replace torn spats that create finger hooks.

Common mistakes

Pockets/zips; filthy unwashed layers; assuming any yoga pant is competition-legal.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What are spats in BJJ?

Compression leggings worn for grappling, especially no-gi.

Do I need shorts over spats?

Many gyms require it—ask your academy.

Gi training?

Optional under pants for skin protection—house preference.

Competition legal?

Event-dependent—verify the attire guide.

Spats vs gi pants?

Spats are stretch no-gi layers; gi pants are kimono trousers.

Beginner buy?

Simple durable pair without pockets/metal.

Related gear?

No-gi shorts and the no-gi hub.

The Bottom Line

Wear spats for coverage and hygiene in no-gi—then confirm shorts-over-spats and event attire rules before you compete.
