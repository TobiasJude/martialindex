Cartwheel Pass

The cartwheel pass is an athletic Brazilian jiu-jitsu open-guard clear that cartwheels over the bottom player’s legs into side control or north-south—part of the speed passing cluster under guard passing.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Use as a high-commitment clear when feet are extended—not as a default white-belt pass.
  • Pillars: open guard top + speed passing.
  • Cluster: jumping pass, toreando, X-pass, float pass.
  • Gi grips can help set the cartwheel; no-gi needs clean wrist control and timing.
  • Failure often means giving up the back or landing in closed guard—have exits.

What Is Cartwheel Pass?

A cartwheel pass vaults over the bottom player’s open-guard feet, rotating hands-to-mat and hips over so you land past the legs. It is spectacular, high-commitment, and situational—popular in highlight reels and certain athletic passing games, not a replacement for toreando fundamentals.

Catalog home: under guard passing, clustered with jumping, X-pass, and toreando against open guard / De La Riva structures.

How Cartwheel Pass Works

Posture and grip so their feet cannot reclaim your hips mid-air, plant hands, cartwheel the hips over the leg line, and land with chest connection into a pin.

Bottom player’s job. Keep feet sticky, elevate into butterfly, catch a single-leg as you commit, or follow the cartwheel to take the back.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Pants grips can freeze one leg before the vault; sleeve control helps posture. Fabric also lets them latch and punish mid-cartwheel.

No-gi. Faster scrambles; cleaner athletic timing required. Pair threats with toreando so the cartwheel is not telegraphed.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Against a stiff standing open guard, the passer fakes toreando, posts, and cartwheels over extended feet into north-south—then stabilizes before hunting submissions.

Same cluster as jumping pass: both are commitment clears when circling passes stall against long legs.

Types / Variations

Over-the-feet cartwheel

Classic vault when both feet are extended and light.

Cartwheel to north-south

Land chest-to-chest north-south, then walk to side control.

Toreando-setup cartwheel

Use toreando/X threats to extend their feet, then vault.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries: extended open-guard feet, failed toreando that leaves light hooks, athletic scramble moments. Counters: sticky feet, back takes, closed-guard recovery. Prefer toreando, X-pass, or pressure options when the vault is low-percentage.

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Legal when executed cleanly. Reckless vaults that endanger the neck/spine can draw penalties under general safety rules—coach judgment matters in class.

Safety

High injury potential if you cartwheel into a stacked opponent or land on your head. Drill progressions; ban uncontrolled cartwheels in beginner rooms.

Common mistakes

  • Cartwheeling into sticky De La Riva without clearing the hook.
  • No landing plan—ending in their closed guard.
  • Using cartwheel as a default instead of toreando fundamentals.
  • Ignoring safer cluster siblings when the vault is obvious.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is a cartwheel pass?

An athletic open-guard clear that cartwheels over the legs into a pin past the hooks.

Should beginners learn it?

Not first. Learn toreando, X-pass, and knee cut; add cartwheel only with coaching.

Cartwheel vs jumping pass?

Cartwheel rotates over the hands; jumping leaps the hips past. Both are high-commitment speed clears.

Does it work in gi?

Yes, but grips can also punish you mid-vault. Use as a surprise after grip breaks.

Main risk?

Giving up the back or landing badly on neck/shoulder. Have a landing pin planned.

When is it high percentage?

When feet are light and extended and you have already threatened circling passes.

Catalog links?

Up to guard passing / open guard / speed passing; across to jumping, toreando, X-pass, float.

The Bottom Line

Cartwheel pass is a situational speed clear—not a pillar technique. Link it to open guard passing and keep toreando/X-pass as your default cluster tools.
