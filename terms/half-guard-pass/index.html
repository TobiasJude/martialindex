Half Guard Pass

Half guard pass is the Martial Index category hub for passing half guard top: clear the trapped leg and win a pin via knee-cut, smash, body lock, hip-switch, knee-shield pass, z-guard pass, HQ, and more.

By Tobias Mesevage · Published 16 Sep 2026 · Updated 16 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Half top is a station—crossface/underhook usually before the extract.
  • Vs shield/z: knee-shield pass, z-guard pass.
  • Vs flat half: knee-cut, smash, body lock, hip-switch.
  • HQ / HQ pass often bridge open → half.
  • Bottom threats: knee-shield sweeps, lockdown, dogfight.

What Is Half Guard Pass?

This is a category hub for passing half guard when you are on top with a leg trapped. Stabilize upper body (crossface, underhook, or body lock), defeat frames (knee shield, z-guard, lockdown), extract the trapped leg, and pin. Parent: half. Umbrellas: guard passing, pressure passing, force half.

How Passing Works Here

Win the shoulder line, flatten or fold their frames, free your trapped knee/foot, and settle side or mount. If they recover butterfly or open, switch hubs.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Underhook + crossface + pants; smash and hip-switch are gi staples.

No-gi. Body-lock and knee-cut metas dominate; HQ entries are constant—see body lock and HQ pass.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Trapped in half → crossface → fold shield → knee-cut to side, or body-lock walk, or hip-switch extract.

Named Pass Menu

Pressure / stack family

Pressure / stack family: smash pass, body-lock pass, over-under pass, pressure passing, connection passing, company pass.

Speed / circling family

Speed / extract family: hip-switch pass, back-step pass, leg staple, standing extract into toreando if they open.

Knee-cut / HQ family

Knee-cut / HQ / shield family: knee-cut, knee-cut to mount, HQ pass, headquarters, knee-shield pass, z-guard pass, force half pass.

When to Choose Which Pass

Vs high shield, fold first (knee-shield pass / z-guard pass). Vs flat half, knee-cut or body-lock. Vs lockdown, free the foot before anything fancy. Bottom answers live on knee-shield sweep, lockdown sweep, old school.

How This Hub Compares

Companion hubs: closed often routes here; open often forces half on purpose (force half).

Special Considerations

Rules & sport context

Standard IBJJF/ADCC; verify illegal twisters/neck pressure in your rule set (IBJJF, ADCC).

Safety

Extract legs without cranking knees; body locks need breathing awareness in training.

Common mistakes

  • Passing without crossface/underhook.
  • Ignoring the knee shield.
  • Celebrating before the trapped foot is free.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is a half guard pass?

The category of clearing a trapped leg from top half into a pin—many named children.

Best first pass from half?

Crossface + knee-cut or body-lock after frames die—are high-percentage starts.

How do I pass knee shield / z-guard?

See the dedicated knee-shield-pass and z-guard-pass pages.

What if they lockdown?

Free the foot before knee-cutting; do not ignore the triangle.

Common mistake?

Driving a knee-cut into a live high shield.

Gi vs no-gi?

Both; no-gi emphasizes body lock and HQ.

Martial Index home?

Under half guard as the pass category hub.

The Bottom Line

Treat half guard pass as a map of live children—knee-cut, body lock, shield pass, z pass, HQ—not a single move.
