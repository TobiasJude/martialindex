Z-Guard Pass

The z-guard pass breaks the high knee shield of z-guard then finishes with knee-slice or smash—clustered with knee-shield pass, z-guard sweeps, and half hub.

By Tobias Mesevage · Published 16 Sep 2026 · Updated 16 Sep 2026

Key Takeaways

  • High shield needs a collapse phase first.
  • Shoulder-to-chin and chest pressure rotate the shin.
  • Then knee-slice or body-lock.
  • Overlaps knee-shield pass.
  • Gi and no-gi.

What Is Z-Guard Pass?

Z-guard pass answers the elevated shin frame near shoulder height. Parent: z-guard. Sibling: knee-shield pass. Hub: half guard pass.

How It Works

Progressively collapse the high shield with shoulder/chest pressure and grip fighting, pin the folded shin, drive a knee-slice across the thigh, free the trapped leg, and settle side control.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Collar grips help the crush.

No-gi. Head and shoulder pressure replace collars.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

High z → crush → knee slice → side.

Types / Variations

Primary pattern

Shoulder-to-chin crush into knee slice.

Competition variation

Body-lock / smash when the shield drops.

Related option

Backstep or jump-the-side when legs turn (advanced).

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries. Vs established z. Counters. Z-guard sweeps, underhooks, rainbow.

How It Compares

Nearly the same toolbox as knee-shield pass with more emphasis on the collapse phase.

Special Considerations

Rules & sport context

Standard half top problem.

Safety

Crush without cervical cranking.

Common mistakes

  • Slicing into an uncollapsed high shield.
  • No crossface.
  • Celebrating before the trail leg frees.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

How do you pass z-guard?

Collapse the high shield, then knee-slice or smash to a pin.

Z vs knee shield?

Z usually means higher shield; menus overlap.

Gi or no-gi?

Both.

What next?

Side/mount via half hub.

Common mistake?

Knee-cutting before the shield folds.

Beginner-friendly?

Intermediate—learn after basic half passing.

Martial Index home?

Under z-guard next to knee-shield pass.

The Bottom Line

Z-guard pass is collapse then cut—drill it with knee-shield pass and study bottom answers.
