Spider Guard Pass

The spider guard pass is the top menu against spider guard—break the feet on biceps, then circle or pressure past—clustered with lasso pass, toreando, and open guard pass.

By Tobias Mesevage · Published 16 Sep 2026 · Updated 16 Sep 2026

Key Takeaways

  • First job: break or neutralize shin-on-bicep grips while standing.
  • Then choose toreando, knee-cut, or smash depending on their reaction.
  • Kneeling into spider is a common beginner trap.
  • Sibling problem: lasso when one foot wraps.
  • Hub context: open guard pass.

What Is Spider Guard Pass?

Spider guard pass covers the sequences that clear feet-on-biceps control. Parent bottom: spider guard. Across top: lasso pass, toreando, bullfighter, knee-cut.

How It Works

Stand with elbows aligned, walk back or knee-behind-heel to pop feet off biceps, control pants, then toreando circle, long-step, or fold into pressure. Do not stay on knees inside extended spider.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Pants and sleeve fights are constant; grip breaks are half the pass.

No-gi. True spider is rare; expect shin frames and wrist rides—same stand-and-clear idea.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

They plant double spider → you stand, extend arms, pop a foot → toreando to side control.

Types / Variations

Primary pattern

Grip-break first, then toreando / bullfighter.

Competition variation

Knee-behind-heel or hip-block grip breaks into knee-cut.

Related option

Vs spider-lasso hybrid—clear spider foot then address lasso.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries. Anytime feet hit biceps. Counters (bottom). Recompose spider, switch to lasso sweep / tripod, invert.

How It Compares

Vs lasso pass: spider is distance feet; lasso is wrapped arm. Both feed open hub.

Special Considerations

Rules & sport context

Gi-heavy problem set in IBJJF; less central in ADCC.

Safety

Do not yank shoulders while ripping grips—control the arm.

Common mistakes

  • Kneeling into extended spider.
  • Passing without breaking grips.
  • Chasing one pass while ignoring the free foot.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

How do you pass spider guard?

Stand, break shin-on-bicep grips, control pants, then toreando, knee-cut, or smash.

Should you pass spider from the knees?

Usually no—standing restores elbow-shoulder alignment.

Gi or no-gi?

Classic spider is gi; no-gi uses frame analogs.

What next after the pass?

Side control or mount—stabilize before submissions.

Common mistake?

Kneeling into the feet or skipping grip breaks.

Beginner-friendly?

Grip-break drills yes; live timing takes reps.

Martial Index home?

Under spider guard inside the open-guard pass mesh.

The Bottom Line

Spider guard pass is grip break then decision—link it to lasso and the open hub.
