IBJJF Gi Requirements

IBJJF gi requirements cover how competition kimonos must fit and present under IBJJF events. Exact measurements change—verify the current IBJJF Rules; this page stays high-level.

By Tobias Mesevage · Published 16 Sep 2026 · Updated 16 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • IBJJF publishes uniform measurement and presentation rules for gi competition.
  • Martial Index does not invent a complete measurement table—numbers go stale.
  • Keep a known-fit competition gi and re-check after washes—see gi sizing.
  • Patches, colors, and belt presentation also matter—read the current guide.
  • Always verify the official rulebook for your event season.

What Are IBJJF Gi Requirements?

IBJJF gi requirements are the federation’s published standards for kimono dimensions, materials/colors as allowed, and related uniform presentation at IBJJF competitions.

How It Works

Athletes may be measured with official tools before competing. Gis that fail can force last-minute changes or prevent matches. Smart competitors verify early.

Gi vs No-Gi

These requirements are gi-specific. No-gi attire has separate guidance in IBJJF no-gi contexts and other promotions.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

At inspection your sleeves are checked; a stretched trainer fails; your backup competition gi passes—you compete instead of panicking.

Types / Variations

Common patterns

Measurement checks; color rules; patch placement limits; belt condition.

Gym / sport variants

Local non-IBJJF events may be looser or use house rules—still read that packet.

Beginner notes

For your first IBJJF, bring two legal-color gis and know belt tying.

How to Apply / Practical Tips

Buy/reserve a competition gi early. Measure at home with the current official method described in the book. Do not cut corners based on forum folklore.

How It Compares

Broader gi sizing includes comfort; IBJJF requirements are the inspection subset.

Special Considerations

Rules & sport context

Verify current rulebook. Do not treat this page as a complete measurement chart. Official source: current IBJJF Rules documentation. Compare other orgs via ADCC Rules when relevant (different attire logic).

Safety

Ill-fitting gis can hinder movement—fit for performance and inspection.

Common mistakes

One stretched gym gi; ignoring patch rules; last-minute borrows that fail length checks.

In the current IBJJF rules documentation for your event season.

No—high-level only so we do not publish stale tables.

How do I prepare?

Keep a known-fit competition gi and verify with the current official method.

Colors allowed?

Event/federation dependent—verify current guide.

Patches?

Often regulated—read the current book.

Treat IBJJF gi requirements as a living official checklist: verify the current rulebook, then bring a gi you already know will pass.
