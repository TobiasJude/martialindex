Truck

The truck is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu control position—central to Eddie Bravo's 10th Planet system—where you entangle both of the opponent's legs and control their hips, creating the platform for the twister, banana split, and related submissions.

By Tobias Mesevage · Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Truck = both legs entangled / controlled from a back-adjacent angle.
  • Gateway to twister and banana-split style attacks.
  • Know your rules—twister often banned in IBJJF.
  • Entered from lockdown, deep half, turtle, and crab-ride scrambles.
  • Related lockdown, crab ride.

What Is the Truck?

The truck is a control where you wrap and control both of their legs (often with your legs and hips) while attacking or threatening their upper body from an angle—famous as the platform for Eddie Bravo's twister. It sits near back-take and lockdown families rather than classic rear mount. It connects tightly to back control.

How the Truck Works

By immobilizing both legs, you remove their hip escape and create spinal/neck and stretch attacks. The twister rotates the spine against controlled hips; banana-split style moves stretch the legs. Without secure leg control, the truck is just a messy side scramble.

Gi vs No-Gi

No-gi / submission-only. Truck's natural home, especially where twisters are legal.

Gi / IBJJF. Position may appear, but twister finishes are typically illegal—use truck for control/transitions only if rules ban the crank.

What Does the Truck Look Like in Practice?

Alex enters truck from lockdown/electric chair when Jordan posts, controls both legs, and—under rules that allow it—attacks the twister. Under IBJJF, Alex instead uses the control to climb toward safer back takes or resets.

Types / Variations

Basic truck control

Both legs controlled, hips immobilized, upper body available.

Twister path

Spinal rotation attack—rule-set dependent.

Banana split / stretches

Leg-separation stretches from the same control family.

Escaping truck (bottom)

Free a leg early, turn into them, recover before the crank starts.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries. Lockdown/electric chair; deep half; turtle; crab-ride scrambles; twister-side control.

Top threats. Twister (where legal), banana split, back takes if they roll out wrong.

Bottom counters. Don't gift both legs; peel early; know legal defenses.

Truck vs Back Control

Factor Truck Back Control
Control focus Both legs entangled Hooks + seatbelt on torso
Signature finish Twister / stretches RNC / gi chokes
IBJJF twister Usually illegal finish RNC legal
Family 10th Planet / lockdown adjacent Universal back game

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Truck control can appear in many rule sets. The twister is generally illegal in IBJJF gi/no-gi at most levels. ADCC and many submission-only shows allow a wider crank set—verify before hunting spinal attacks. Banana-split style stretches also vary by event.

Safety

Twisters and spinal cranks are serious. Only train under qualified coaches with partners who know how to tap. Never crank cold truck attacks in open mat horseplay.

Common mistakes

  • Hunting twister under IBJJF rules.
  • Loose leg control— they simply turn out.
  • Cranking without control or communication in drilling.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

Is the twister legal in IBJJF?

Generally no at standard IBJJF levels. Treat twister as a submission-only / specific-rules attack unless your event sheet says otherwise.

Truck vs crab ride?

Crab ride climbs to classic back control; truck entangles both legs for twister/stretch paths. See crab ride.

How do I enter the truck?

Commonly from lockdown/electric chair, deep half, turtle, or twister-side control—ask your coach for the entries legal in your gym's rules.

Can I use truck without the twister?

Yes—as control and as a path to safer back takes when cranks are banned.

Is truck only 10th Planet?

10th Planet systematized and named it widely; similar leg-entangled controls appear elsewhere.

How do I escape the truck?

Free at least one leg immediately, turn into the attacker, and recover before spinal rotation starts. Tap early if the crank begins.

The Bottom Line

The truck entangles both legs as a platform for twister and stretch attacks—know your rules before cranking. See lockdown and crab ride.
