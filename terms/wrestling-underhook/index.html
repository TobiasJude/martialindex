What Is Underhook (Wrestling)?

The underhook is inside position: your arm under their armpit, hand on the back or shoulder, hip pressure close.

This page is Underhook (Wrestling) because BJJ underhook already covers BJJ framing—compare when useful.

Underhooks feed Body Lock, Duck Under, Fireman's Carry, and shot lanes when you lift the elbow.

In Wrestling, underhooks are Greco gold and freestyle/folkstyle daily currency; the opposite defense is often the Whizzer.

Martial Index files Underhook (Wrestling) under Wrestling so searchers get definition, mechanics, and live batch links—not a thin glossary stub.

Read this beside the Wrestling style hub when you need the art’s big picture; keep the focus on how Underhook (Wrestling) behaves across freestyle, folkstyle, and Greco-Roman contexts.

How Underhook (Wrestling) Works

Pummel: clear their elbow, shoot your arm deep under the armpit, and connect your hip so the underhook is structural, not decorative.

Head position matters—do not give a front headlock while you dig.

Offense: turn the corner for Duck Under, lock body for throws, or clear into a High Crotch.

If they whizzer hard, adjust height, circle, or abandon into another tie rather than fighting a stalled underhook forever.

Mechanically, successful underhook (wrestling) work is usually a chain: Wrestling Stance integrity → Level Change → entry or control → finish or recovery.

If stance collapses or the Penetration Step stalls short, even textbook grips fail—too high and you meet a Sprawl (Wrestling); too loose and you invite a Whizzer or scramble.

Pair offense with the defensive layer this tool implies: shots need sprawl literacy; ties need hand-fighting; pins need Near Fall awareness in folkstyle.

Rules / Style Notes

Under United World Wrestling freestyle rules, underhook (wrestling) is judged through takedowns, exposure, and activity—not riding time.

Under NCAA/NFHS folkstyle, control, escapes, reversals, and near falls reshape how underhook (wrestling) is valued once the wrestlers hit the mat.

Greco-Roman bans holds below the waist, so leg-attack cousins of underhook (wrestling) either disappear or migrate into upper-body variants.

Confirm the rule set before importing gym habits into contest intensity—folkstyle riding does not equal freestyle par terre.

Avoid illegal hands on the spine or face while fighting underhooks.

Clinch stalling rules still apply.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Greco pummel battle for underhooks before any throw attempt.

Folkstyle: underhook to high-C when the near elbow floats.

Match picture: underhook (wrestling) appears when stance, ties, and timing say so, then yields to a finish, a Go-Behind, or a reset to neutral.

Room test: can you apply underhook (wrestling) after a Collar Tie or Russian Tie exchange and still recover posture? If not, chain it instead of isolating forever.

Common Mistakes

  • Shallow underhook with hips away.
  • Digging your head down into a snap.
  • Ignoring the whizzer.
  • No follow-up attack—just holding.
  • Treating underhook (wrestling) as a highlight instead of a stance-and-timing skill.
  • Shooting or turning without a recovery plan when the opponent sprawls or bridges.
  • Ignoring sibling tools that set or cover the technique.

Study Notes

Coaches often describe underhook (wrestling) as “simple to show, hard to own.” The gap is usually not a missing secret—it is missing reps under hand-fighting stress, a collapsed Wrestling Stance, or a Level Change that never gets under the opponent’s hips.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

Underhook vs overhook?

Underhook is under the armpit; overhook/Whizzer wraps over the arm—often the counter.

Why wrestling-prefixed?

Underhook (BJJ) is the BJJ page; this entry is wrestling-specific.

Greco importance?

Critical—leg attacks are gone, so underhooks decide many exchanges.

How to get it?

Pummel and elbow clear; do not reach from far away.

Best attacks?

Body Lock, Duck Under, Fireman's Carry, High Crotch.

Defense when they have it?

Whizzer, hip in, and clear.

The Bottom Line

An underhook is only as good as your hip connection—win inside position, then cash it before the whizzer freezes the exchange.
