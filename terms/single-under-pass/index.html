Single-Under Pass

The single-under pass threads one arm under a thigh, stacks the hips, and walks around when double unders will not fit—clustered with stack pass, closed hub, and over-under.

By Tobias Mesevage · Published 16 Sep 2026 · Updated 16 Sep 2026

Key Takeaways

  • One deep under + opposite-side control.
  • Stack toward the shoulders, walk around the underhooked leg.
  • Keep the free elbow safe from triangles.
  • Classic after opening closed guard.
  • Gi and no-gi.

What Is Single-Under Pass?

Single-under (single underhook stack) is a pressure pass with one thigh underhook. Parent problem sets: closed, open. Siblings: double-under, stack, over-under. Hub: closed guard pass.

How It Works

Open the guard or elevate one leg, shoot a deep underhook under that thigh, control the far side with crossface/overhook/hip pin, stack, walk around the underhooked leg, and pin side control only after the knee line clears.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Collar grips assist the far-side pin.

No-gi. Head pressure and wrist control replace collars.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Closed opens → single under on the near thigh → stack → walk to side.

Types / Variations

Primary pattern

Classic single-under stack from closed/open.

Competition variation

Switch from failed double-under when the second arm cannot thread.

Related option

Combine with toreando to force the elevated leg, then drop the under.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries. After opening closed; from open elevation. Counters. Triangle threats if your free elbow sneaks inside; reverse stack; invert.

How It Compares

Vs double-under: only one under. Vs over-under: over-under mixes under and over on opposite sides by design.

Special Considerations

Rules & sport context

Fundamental IBJJF pressure pass.

Safety

Stack without cervical dumping—drive hips, not neck.

Common mistakes

  • Free elbow inside (triangle).
  • Shallow underhook.
  • Releasing before the knee clears.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is the single-under pass?

A stack pass using one deep thigh underhook and opposite-side control.

When not double-under?

When the second under will not fit—single-under is the practical sibling.

Triangle risk?

Keep the free elbow outside their far knee.

Gi or no-gi?

Both.

Common mistake?

Shallow under or free elbow inside.

Beginner-friendly?

Yes—core pressure curriculum.

Martial Index home?

Under closed-guard-pass hub next to double-under and stack.

The Bottom Line

Single-under pass is practical stack pressure—drill it beside double-under and the closed hub.
