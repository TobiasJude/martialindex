Maintain Side Control

Maintain side control keeps the side control pin alive against frame and shrimp—crossface the head, kill the near hip, stay heavy and mobile—then advance under positional hierarchy.

Published 12 Sep 2026 · Updated 12 Sep 2026

Key Takeaways

  • Head + near hip are the hinges.
  • Hips heavy; knees light.
  • Follow bridges into north-south.
  • Collapse frames before you attack.
  • Climb only when the pin is quiet.

What Is Maintain Side Control?

Retention skill after pass to side.

Primary bottom answer is frame-and-shrimp / recover guard—your job is to deny the pocket.

Sibling to maintain mount and maintain back.

How Maintain Side Control Works

Crossface to turn their face away; underhook or block the far side.

Glue near knee to their hip; erase shrimp space.

Drive weight through toes/shoulders, not parked knees.

When they bridge, switch to north-south or re-angle rather than fighting flat.

Collapse frames, then choose mount, KOB, or submissions.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Crossface collar and far underhook are sticky.

No-gi. Shoulder pressure and underhooks; expect more bridging.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Lee’s partner shrimps; Lee follows to north-south, then returns with a tighter crossface.

Pat kills the near elbow frame, then steps toward mount.

Types / Variations

Classic crossface pin

Head + hip.

Mobile cycle

Side ↔ NS ↔ KOB.

Scarf / modified scarf

When they give the head.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

After any side-control landing. Bottom: frames, shrimp, underhook turns. Top: maintain, then pass to mount or attack.

Maintain Side vs Maintain Mount

Factor Maintain side Maintain mount
Primary escape faced Frame-and-shrimp Upa + elbow escape
Control focus Head and near hip Base and hip follow
Next step Mount / back / KOB Submissions / technical mount
Beginner error Static knees, open hip Arms trapped for upa

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Holding side control protects pass points and sets up mount scoring—verify current IBJJF / ADCC rules.

Safety

Shoulder pressure can choke—communicate in training; ease off if partners can’t breathe.

Common mistakes

  • Kneeling with no weight.
  • Ignoring the near hip.
  • Staying static through bridges.
  • Attacking while frames are alive.

Related notes

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is maintain side control?

Keeping the side pin against escapes.

Key controls?

Head (crossface) and near hip.

Vs shrimp?

Erase the pocket; follow the bridge.

When to climb?

When frames are dead and hips are quiet.

Gi tool?

Crossface collar.

Sibling concepts?

Maintain mount / maintain back.

Bottom’s page?

Frame and shrimp.

The Bottom Line

Maintain side control is own the hub—head, hip, heavy hips—then climb or finish without donating the pass.
