What Is Palding (Trunk Scoring)?

Palding (팔딩 / trunk scoring slang in sport talk) points at body-point opportunities on the protector.

It is how coaches talk about “scoring the body” under World Taekwondo Rules rather than only hunting head kicks.

Compare with Hogu: hogu is the equipment; palding is the scoring concept aimed at that equipment.

Volume to palding often sets up head-kick attempts.

Martial Index files Palding (Trunk Scoring) under Taekwondo so searchers get definition, mechanics, WT/ITF context, and live batch links—not a thin glossary stub.

How Palding (Trunk Scoring) Works

Identify legal trunk panels, choose techniques (Dollyo Chagi (Turning Kick), Dwi Chagi (Back Kick), etc.) that meet angle/force needs, and recover.

PSS thresholds teach athletes to kick through, not tap.

Combine with cuts (Cut Kick (Taekwondo)) so opponents cannot sit outside forever.

Track which of your techniques actually beep in practice gear.

Mechanically, successful palding (trunk scoring) work is usually a chain: stance integrity → timing → impact or control → recovery to Taekwondo Stance with hands alive.

If distance is wrong, even perfect form fails—too far and you kick air; too close and you collide into clinches or jammed chambers you did not plan.

Pair offense with the defensive layer this tool implies: kicks need Arae Makki (Low Block)/Momtong Makki (Middle Block) literacy when drills reverse roles; sparring tools need Hogu and Electronic Scoring (PSS) awareness.

WT / ITF / Rules Notes

Under World Taekwondo (Olympic) competition, palding (trunk scoring) is interpreted through World Taekwondo Rules, Kyorugi (Sparring) scoring zones, and often Electronic Scoring (PSS)—not bare gym aesthetics.

ITF Taekwondo schools may share Korean vocabulary while differing on sine-wave motion, hand techniques, and pattern (tul) emphasis; do not assume WT footage equals ITF syllabus.

Gym culture may drill palding (trunk scoring) daily; contest night may shrink the toolkit based on weight class, PSS sensor thresholds, and coaching strategy.

Confirm the event agreement (WT, ITF, open, recreational) before importing sparring volume into contest intensity.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Round plan: establish palding early, force shelling, then go upstairs.

Film review: many “hard” kicks miss sensors because they glance the arm, not the trunk zone.

Fight picture: palding (trunk scoring) appears when range and timing say so, then yields to the next kick or exit back to Taekwondo Stance.

Gym test: can you apply palding (trunk scoring) after a feint and still recover guard and balance? If not, connect it to combinations instead of isolating forever.

Common Mistakes

  • Ignoring body points while only throwing highlight heads.
  • Kicking arms and calling it bad luck.
  • Not training in a real {hogu}.
  • Predictable single-kick rhythm.
  • Forgetting penalties while chasing beeps.
  • Treating palding (trunk scoring) as a highlight instead of a range-and-timing skill.
  • Skipping recovery—landing then posing with hands down.
  • Ignoring sibling tools that set or cover the technique.

Training Notes

Shadowkick palding (trunk scoring) inside full rounds; return to Taekwondo Stance after every attempt.

Pads: technical → moving → forced follow-up so palding (trunk scoring) connects to a combination.

Partner drills beat bag-only fluency; feed realistic rhythm so counter timing is part of the skill.

Film sparring weekly and ask whether palding (trunk scoring) appears in the right range band for Kyorugi (Sparring) or forms context.

Progress intensity with a coach—especially for spinning kicks, axe kicks, and PSS-targeted Palding (Trunk Scoring) work.

Study Notes

Coaches often describe palding (trunk scoring) as “simple to show, hard to own.” The gap is usually not a missing secret—it is missing reps under stress, poor distance, or a stance that collapses when pressure arrives.

When you compare gyms, you will see stylistic accents: some put palding (trunk scoring) behind heavy Olympic pressure kicking; others behind traditional Poomsae precision. The encyclopedia definition stays stable even when accents change.

Use internal links in this entry as a study path—move from pillars like Taekwondo Stance, Ap Chagi (Front Kick), and Dollyo Chagi (Turning Kick) into siblings, then return here after sparring to re-read mistakes with fresh context.

Safety note: encyclopedia pages are reference material, not live coaching. Train under qualified instructors, especially for spinning kicks and head-height targets where mistiming injures partners.

Keep vocabulary precise. Fighters overload words like “roundhouse,” “cut kick,” and “axe.” Martial Index’s Taekwondo entries keep palding (trunk scoring) scoped so you are not dumped into unrelated Muay Thai articles that share casual English labels.

A practical weekly plan: two technical rounds focused on palding (trunk scoring), one situational sparring theme that forces it, and one film review asking where timing failed.

If you compete, write a one-line rule note for palding (trunk scoring) under your next card (WT vs ITF, PSS vs paper scoring, headgear) so training intensity matches the bout.
