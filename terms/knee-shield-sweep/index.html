Knee Shield Sweep

The knee shield sweep is the offensive menu from knee-shield half guard—John Wayne / knee-lever, arm drag, and butterfly-hook reverses—clustered with z-guard, knee lever sweep, and knee shield pass.

By Tobias Mesevage · Published 16 Sep 2026 · Updated 16 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Knee shield is a frame; sweeps appear when you convert the frame into off-balance.
  • John Wayne / knee lever is the flagship branch.
  • Arm-drag and butterfly-hook sweeps appear when they fight the arms.
  • Top answers: knee shield pass, smash, body lock.
  • Works gi and no-gi with grip changes.

What Is Knee Shield Sweep?

Knee shield sweep names the reverses launched while your shin frames across their chest from half guard. Parent position: knee-shield half guard (closely related to z-guard). Across: knee lever, old school, butterfly sweep.

How It Works

Maintain the shield, win a collar/underhook or 2-on-1, then either lever their knee line (John Wayne), arm-drag to the back/dogfight, or insert a butterfly hook and elevate.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Collar + sleeve or pants help the John Wayne lever.

No-gi. Underhooks, wrist control, and head position replace collars.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

They pressure into your shield → you 2-on-1 their arm → knee lever dumps them → you come up to top half or side.

Types / Variations

Primary pattern

John Wayne / knee-lever from high or mid shield.

Competition variation

Arm-drag to back or dogfight when they reach.

Related option

Butterfly-hook sweep when you can insert the free hook.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries. Recover to shield from flat half; from open guard. Counters. Pass the shield, z-guard pass, smash.

How It Compares

Vs z-guard sweep: overlapping; z often implies higher shield geometry. Vs lockdown sweep: lockdown triangulates the leg instead of framing the chest.

Special Considerations

Rules & sport context

Standard IBJJF/ADCC half-guard offense; verify current rules on IBJJF rules.

Safety

Do not hyperextend their knee with a reckless lever—control the dump.

Common mistakes

  • Shield too soft (they smash through).
  • Sweeping without upper-body control.
  • Turning away and giving the back.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is a knee shield sweep?

Any reverse launched from knee-shield half—especially John Wayne/knee-lever, arm-drag, and butterfly-hook branches.

Is John Wayne the same as knee lever?

Often used interchangeably for the lever-style dump from shield/half.

How do you set it up?

Keep the shin frame, win upper-body control, then lever, drag, or insert a butterfly hook.

What should you chain next?

Top half, side, dogfight, or back.

Common mistake?

Soft shield or sweeping without grips/underhooks.

Is it beginner-friendly?

Yes—core half-guard curriculum.

Where does it fit on Martial Index?

Under knee-shield half guard next to z-guard and knee-lever sweep.

The Bottom Line

Knee shield sweep turns a defensive shin frame into offense—drill John Wayne first, then arm-drag and butterfly branches, and study the pass answers.
