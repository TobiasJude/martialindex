Tripod Sweep

The tripod sweep is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu open-guard reverse that attacks a standing opponent's three-point (tripod) base - often from De La Riva or shin-to-shin - dumping them to come on top.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Off-balance the standing base, remove a post, reverse to top.
  • Pillar: open guard / DLR.
  • Cluster: tomoe, hook, butterfly; top answers toreando/X/knee cut.
  • Gi pants/sleeve help; no-gi uses ankle rides and sleeve analogs.
  • Chain with technical stand-up when the scramble stands both up.

What Is Tripod Sweep?

Tripod sweep targets a standing passer who is posted on two feet and a hand (or narrow base). You remove a post and dump them - modern open-guard versions are high percentage.

Up: open guard, De La Riva, shin-to-shin. Across: tomoe, hook, X-pass / toreando as top answers.

How Tripod Sweep Works

Control ankle and sleeve/collar (or wrists), load one of their posts, kick or pull through to remove the tripod, and follow to mount/side.

Top answers: keep four points of base, toreando clear, X-pass, or long-step before the dump.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Pant and sleeve grips freeze the tripod.

No-gi. Ankle control + wrist rides; timing sharper.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

DLR hook + sleeve to off-balance to tripod dump to top. If they hop the base out, switch to tomoe or butterfly elevate.

Types / Variations

De La Riva tripod

Classic DLR-fed dump.

Shin-to-shin tripod

From shin-to-shin open guard.

Modern open-guard tripod details

Hidden posts and ankle rides.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries: DLR, shin-to-shin, standing open guard. Counters: wide base, toreando, X-pass. Link technical stand-up when both stand.

Tripod vs Tomoe Nage Sweep

Factor Tripod Tomoe Nage
Commitment Usually non-sacrifice Sacrifice roll
Pillar Open guard / DLR Open guard / DLR
Best vs Standing narrow base Forward-loaded posture
Beginner? Often yes with drilling Needs breakfalls

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Legal. High-percentage open-guard sport sweep.

Safety

Control the dump so partners fall safely. Clear mats when drilling standing.

Common mistakes

  • Attacking a wide four-point base.
  • No follow to top.
  • Ignoring toreando/X top answers.
  • Orphaning from open-guard pillar.

Related notes

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is the tripod sweep?

A BJJ sweep that reverses top/bottom using the tripod sweep mechanics described above.

Gi or no-gi?

Both, with different grips. Gi uses sleeves/pants/collar; no-gi uses underhooks, head control, and hooks.

Where does it sit in the catalog?

Under open guard / De La Riva; across to tomoe, hook, toreando.

Good for white belts?

Often yes when drilled slowly with a clear hip path—confirm with your coach for your body type.

What if it fails?

Chain to a sibling sweep or attack (armbar/triangle/kimura) instead of forcing one path.

Why tripod?

You attack a three-point standing base - remove a post and they fall.

Tripod vs tomoe?

Tripod is usually less sacrificial; see tomoe.

The Bottom Line

Tripod sweep is open-guard standing literacy: break the tripod, come up. Link open guard and DLR; keep tomoe/hook and toreando/X in the mesh.
