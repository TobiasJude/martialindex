Figure-Four RNC

The figure-four RNC is the textbook rear naked choke finish: the choking hand catches your opposite bicep, the support hand hides behind their head, and you join the elbows so both carotids meet your forearm and biceps. It is the default once the setup from back has won the chin.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026

Key Takeaways

  • Bicep catch first, then the behind-the-head hand—not a wild hug.
  • Finish by expanding the chest and intersecting the elbows, not by pulling the head off.
  • Sibling grips: palm-to-palm (faster) and short choke (shallower).
  • Hide thumbs. Visible fingers get peeled.
  • Works identically in gi and no-gi; gi just adds collar backups.

What Is Figure-Four RNC?

When coaches say “do a proper RNC,” they mean this figure-four. The choking arm is a V around the neck; the support arm completes a second V behind the head. Together they create bilateral carotid pressure without needing to crank the jaw.

It takes one more step than a palm-to-palm clasp, which is why scrambles sometimes never get here. Against a defending partner who is already controlled, the figure-four is the highest-percentage finish in the family.

Martial Index gives the grip its own page so “figure four rear naked” can rank, then sends you to the parent RNC and the setup page for everything that is not the lock itself.

How Figure-Four RNC Works

After the choking arm is under the chin, place that hand on your opposite biceps (not on your own shoulder). Slide the support hand behind their head—palm to skull, fingers hidden.

Draw the elbows toward each other. Crunch. Expand the chest. Optional: a little rotation as taught in modern “tighten the RNC” details. Do not extend your body away from them.

If the bicep is unreachable, clasp palm-to-palm or switch to a short choke rather than leaving a floating hand in space.

Keep hooks or a body triangle. A perfect figure-four with no lower-body connection is still a back-loss waiting to happen.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Same finish. Hair and collars can snag the behind-the-head hand—slice the hand in palm-toward-you.

No-gi. The default high-percentage finish from a stable back.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Alex wins the chin, catches the bicep, hides the second hand, and taps with a chest expansion instead of a yank.

Jordan cannot reach the bicep, clasps palm-to-palm, then converts to figure-four when space appears.

Types / Variations

Standard figure-four

Bicep + hand behind head. The picture in every fundamentals class.

With body triangle

Lower-body lock plus figure-four—very hard to shake.

One-arm / rotational assist

Extra rotation when the second hand is being fought—still aim to complete the figure-four.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

You only choose this grip after a successful setup. Counters: peel the behind-the-head hand, split the bicep catch, chin down early. Escapes after it closes are rare—tap.

Figure-Four vs Palm-to-Palm RNC

Factor Figure-four Palm-to-palm
Control Best head control Faster, less skull control
When Stable back, time to lock Scramble / short limbs
Peel defense Hide both hands Hide thumbs, convert if peeled
Teaching default Yes Backup / MMA clasp

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Adult rear naked chokes are standard legal chokes. Always verify the current IBJJF and ADCC (or event) rulebooks for division, belt, age, and gi vs no-gi. Martial Index gives orientation—not invented illegal-move lists.

Safety

This should be a blood choke. If you are only grinding the windpipe or twisting the head, reset. Partners tap to pressure, not to ego.

Common mistakes

  • Pulling the head backward like a lever.
  • Grabbing your own shoulder instead of the bicep.
  • Fingers splayed on the skull for them to peel.
  • No hooks—beautiful choke, lost back.

Related notes

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

Is this just “the RNC”?

It is the standard finish of the RNC. Other grips are variants.

Hand on bicep or forearm?

Bicep. Forearm catches leak.

Why behind the head?

It stops the chin tucking back out and completes the vise.

Chest expansion cue?

Yes—become wide, then crunch. Do not stretch them like a torture rack.

Short arms?

Get deeper first; if the bicep is impossible, use palm-to-palm or short choke.

Gi hair problems?

Slide the support hand palm-in so it does not snag.

Legal?

Standard adult choke—verify your current book.

The Bottom Line

Figure-four RNC is the textbook close: bicep catch, hidden hand behind the head, elbows together. Use palm-to-palm when you cannot complete it and setup from back when you never get the chin.
