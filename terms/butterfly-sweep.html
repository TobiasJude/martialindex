Butterfly Sweep

The butterfly sweep is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu reverse from butterfly guard that elevates with inside hooks and pulls the top player over—often into mount—clustered with hook sweep and elevator.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Elevate with a hook, off-balance upper body, come up on top.
  • Pillar: butterfly guard (also touches open/seated).
  • Cluster: hook sweep, elevator, float pass (top answers), technical standup exits.
  • Gi grips help; no-gi underhooks are classic Marcelo language.
  • Failed sweep → take the back or re-elevate—do not go flat.

What Is Butterfly Sweep?

Butterfly sweep uses inside hooks (butterfly hooks) to elevate the top player's hips while upper-body control pulls them over your shoulder line into a reverse. It is the signature attack of butterfly guard.

Up: butterfly, open guard, seated guard. Across: hook sweep, elevator, tripod; top cluster float / folding.

How Butterfly Sweep Works

Win underhook or collar/sleeve, load a hook under one thigh, elevate while leaning them over the hook, and come up past their posts into mount or side.

Top answers: float pass, fold/smash, body lock, kick the hook free—see Batch 4 float and folding passes.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Collar/sleeve + pant assist elevation.

No-gi. Underhook + head control; hooks must be snappy.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Seated butterfly → underhook → elevate → mount. If they sprawl, climb the back or switch to hook/elevator angles.

Types / Variations

Classic butterfly to mount

Hook elevate + underhook pull.

Overhead / heave variations

More vertical elevation when they are light.

Butterfly sweep to back

When they turn away from the elevate.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries: seated butterfly, failed closed-guard open to butterfly, half-to-butterfly recovery. Counters: float, fold, kick hooks. Chain to hook/elevator.

Butterfly Sweep vs Hook Sweep

Factor Butterfly Sweep Hook Sweep
Hooks Inside butterfly hooks Often outside/leg-hook angles
Pillar Butterfly guard Butterfly / open / lasso contexts
Feel Elevate and dump Hook and pull/rotate
Top answer cluster Float / fold Float / toreando / X

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Legal. Core sport and ADCC butterfly game.

Safety

Elevate with control—do not launch partners onto their neck. Mat space awareness when heaving overhead.

Common mistakes

  • Elevating without upper-body control.
  • Going flat after a failed elevate.
  • Ignoring float/fold top answers when studying both sides.
  • Orphaning from butterfly pillar.

Related notes

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is the butterfly sweep?

A BJJ sweep that reverses top/bottom using the butterfly sweep mechanics described above.

Gi or no-gi?

Both, with different grips. Gi uses sleeves/pants/collar; no-gi uses underhooks, head control, and hooks.

Where does it sit in the catalog?

Under butterfly guard; across to hook, elevator, float pass.

Good for white belts?

Often yes when drilled slowly with a clear hip path—confirm with your coach for your body type.

What if it fails?

Chain to a sibling sweep or attack (armbar/triangle/kimura) instead of forcing one path.

Marcelo butterfly sweep?

Marcelo popularized high-percentage no-gi butterfly underhook sweeps; principles transfer to gi.

Butterfly vs elevator?

Elevator often stresses lifting with hooks from closed/open; butterfly is the seated-hook system. See elevator.

The Bottom Line

Butterfly sweep is butterfly guard's engine: hook, elevate, reverse. Link the butterfly pillar and keep hook/elevator/float in the same cluster map.
