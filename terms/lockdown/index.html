Lockdown

Lockdown is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu half-guard control—central to Eddie Bravo's 10th Planet system—where you figure-four their trapped leg with your legs to immobilize them, set the electric chair, and slow smash passes in no-gi.

By Tobias Mesevage · Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Figure-four on their trapped leg = lockdown.
  • 10th Planet native: control first, then electric chair / twister-side paths.
  • Stronger in no-gi; gi players still use it with pants adjustments.
  • Can stress knees—communicate and drill carefully.
  • Parent half guard; often pairs with rubber guard systems.

What Is Lockdown?

Lockdown is a half-guard entanglement where you wrap their trapped leg in a figure-four with your own legs—typically one foot hooked behind their knee/calf while your other leg locks over your ankle—pinning their limb and limiting their ability to free the knee or smash through. It is the signature control of Eddie Bravo's 10th Planet half-guard game.

It belongs to half guard, alongside knee shield and deep half, but emphasizes immobilization and submission setups over classic underhook dogfights.

How Lockdown Works

The figure-four removes their base on that side and creates leverage for hip escapes, electric chair stretches, and transitions toward twister side or truck. From a secured lockdown you can force them to post, then attack the far leg/hip line. Control quality matters: a loose lockdown is just half guard with extra steps.

Top side. Stay heavy on the free side, free your knee carefully, avoid feeding the electric chair, and extract without giving up the back.

Gi vs No-Gi

No-gi. Lockdown's natural home—rashguards do not snag the figure-four the way gis can, and 10th Planet systems were built without cloth.

Gi. Still usable, but pants grips change entries and some electric-chair mechanics feel different. Many gi athletes prefer shield/underhook half instead.

MMA. Lockdown can slow top pressure and create submission threats; manage punches while entangled.

What Does Lockdown Look Like in Practice?

Alex catches Jordan in half, figure-fours the trapped leg into lockdown, stretches into electric chair until Jordan posts, then climbs toward the back or truck.

No-gi. Clean lockdown and electric chair—classic 10th Planet sequence.

Gi. Alex may use pants to help lock the figure-four, then choose a more traditional sweep if the electric chair is stuffed.

Types / Variations

Basic lockdown figure-four

Standard ankle-behind-knee lock used to freeze their leg and start offense.

Electric chair path

Stretch and elevate from lockdown to force posts and open submission/sweep finishes.

Twister side / truck entries

When they defend the chair, scoop toward twister side control or truck for neck/spinal attacks (rule-set dependent).

Escaping lockdown (top)

Extract the knee, clear the figure-four, and pass without feeding back exposure.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries. From half when the shield fails; from rubber-guard systems that dump into half; deliberately pulling lockdown against smash passers.

Bottom threats. Electric chair, banana split variants, truck/twister paths, back takes.

Top counters. Prevent the figure-four early, free the knee, stay heavy, refuse the far-leg feed.

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Lockdown itself is legal. Electric chair and banana-split style stretches are usually legal; twister and certain neck cranks are restricted or banned depending on the organization—check IBJJF vs ADCC vs local rules before hunting spinal attacks.

Safety

Lockdown and electric chair can load knees and hips. Drill progressively, communicate, and tap early. Never crank twisters on untrained partners outside appropriate rules.

Common mistakes

  • Loose figure-four that does not actually immobilize.
  • Hunting electric chair without establishing control first.
  • Ignoring punches/pressure in MMA while locked down.
  • Forcing twister paths in IBJJF gi without knowing the rules.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

Is lockdown only for 10th Planet?

No, but 10th Planet systematized it. Many no-gi gyms teach lockdown as a half-guard tool.

Does lockdown work in the gi?

Yes, with adjustments. Many gi athletes still prefer knee shield and underhook half as defaults.

What is the electric chair?

A stretch/elevation attack from lockdown that forces posts and opens sweeps or submissions on the far side.

Is the twister legal in IBJJF?

Generally no at most IBJJF levels—treat twister/truck neck cranks as ADCC/submission-only topics unless your rules clearly allow them.

How do I escape lockdown on top?

Free your knee carefully, clear the figure-four, stay heavy on their upper body, and avoid feeding the electric chair.

Lockdown vs deep half?

Lockdown figure-fours for control and chair attacks; deep half cradles across for waiters and backs. See deep half.

The Bottom Line

Lockdown figure-fours their trapped leg—10th Planet's half-guard control for electric chair and truck-side attacks. Native to no-gi; usable in gi with care. See half guard and truck.
