Tomoe Nage Sweep

The tomoe nage sweep adapts the judo sacrifice throw into BJJ as a foot-in-hip reverse from open guard or De La Riva, rolling them over to finish on top.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Foot in hip, pull, sacrifice, roll them over - come up controlled.
  • Pillars: open guard / DLR.
  • Cluster: tripod, hook; standing resets via technical stand-up.
  • Gi lapel/sleeve help; no-gi needs wrist control.
  • High commitment - drill breakfalls.

What Is Tomoe Nage Sweep?

Tomoe nage in BJJ is usually a sacrifice reverse: foot on hip, pull with grips, roll to your back, send them over, follow to top. Up: open guard, De La Riva, guard.

Across: tripod, hook, technical stand-up.

How Tomoe Nage Sweep Works

Control sleeves/lapels or wrists, plant the foot, load them forward, sacrifice and circle them over, follow to mount/side.

Top answers: keep hips heavy, step offline, sprawl, or pass as they sacrifice.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Classic lapel/sleeve tomoe.

No-gi. Wrist and head control; timing critical.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

From DLR, pull them onto a foot-in-hip tomoe and roll to top. Prefer tripod as a lower-commitment cousin for beginners.

Types / Variations

Open-guard / DLR tomoe

Foot-in-hip from bottom open guard.

Standing clinch tomoe

Closer to judo entry, finished as BJJ top.

Tomoe into submission scramble

Triangle/armbar during/after the roll.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries: DLR pulls, standing grip fights, failed tripod. Counters: heavy base, step around.

Tomoe Nage vs Tripod Sweep

Factor Tomoe Nage Tripod
Commitment Sacrifice roll Usually less sacrificial
Pillar Open guard / DLR Open guard
Heritage Judo throw adapted Modern BJJ open-guard staple
Risk Landing under them if failed Getting passed if hooks light

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Legal as a sweep/throw when controlled.

Safety

High - progressive breakfalls and coach oversight required.

Common mistakes

  • No grips before the foot plant.
  • Not following to top.
  • Using as white-belt default without ukemi.
  • Missing open-guard pillar links.

Related notes

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is the tomoe nage sweep?

A BJJ sweep that reverses top/bottom using the tomoe nage sweep mechanics described above.

Gi or no-gi?

Both, with different grips. Gi uses sleeves/pants/collar; no-gi uses underhooks, head control, and hooks.

Where does it sit in the catalog?

Under open guard / De La Riva; across to tripod, hook, technical stand-up.

Good for white belts?

Often yes when drilled slowly with a clear hip path—confirm with your coach for your body type.

What if it fails?

Chain to a sibling sweep or attack (armbar/triangle/kimura) instead of forcing one path.

Is it judo or BJJ?

Judo throw adapted as a BJJ sweep/reverse.

Safe for beginners?

Only with breakfalls. Prefer tripod/scissor first.

The Bottom Line

Tomoe nage sweep is a sacrifice reverse from open guard/DLR. Link open guard and keep tripod as the lower-commitment cousin.
