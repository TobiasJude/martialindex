Knee Slice

The knee slice is the Brazilian jiu-jitsu guard pass that drives a shin/knee across the bottom player’s near thigh and “slices” through to side control. Martial Index treats it as the same family as the live knee cut pass—with a teaching accent on the slicing finish rather than the whole headquarters system.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Same family as knee-cut-pass: slice = finish accent; knee cut = systems name on Martial Index.
  • Pillars: guard passing + headquarters / half top.
  • Cluster: hip switch, force half, long step, toreando, X-pass.
  • Win upper-body connection before the slice—or get underhooked.
  • Gi: collar/crossface glue; no-gi: head pressure and underhook fights.

What Is Knee Slice?

Knee slice, knee cut, and cross-knee through describe one splitting-pass family: pin a leg, place the near knee across the thigh, and drive past the knee line into a pin. On Martial Index, knee-cut-pass is the canonical systems entry (headquarters, entries, failures). Knee slice is the synonym-forward entry that emphasizes the slicing finish language many gyms and search queries use. It sits inside the broader guard passing family.

They are not rival techniques. If you already studied knee-cut-pass, treat this page as the slice-accent twin with cluster links into Batch 4 half/speed passes.

How Knee Slice Works

Control the near leg, place the cutting knee on the thigh, win crossface/underhook/collar drive, and slice the hips past while keeping their shoulders flat—same mechanics detailed on the knee-cut page.

Nuance. “Slice” coaching often stresses continuous shin pressure through the thigh (like cutting) rather than pausing in headquarters. “Cut” curricula often stress HQ as a decision node among cut, long-step, and smash.

Bottom player’s job. Underhook early, recover half, invert, or frame the hip before the shin fully crosses—answers shared with knee-cut retention.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Collar and sleeve help flatten during the slice; pants grips pin the far leg. Lasso/spider frames try to stop the cut before it starts.

No-gi. Head position and underhook battles dominate. Pair with body lock and toreando threats so the slice is not a single read.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

From headquarters after a toreando or force-half entry, the passer slices the near knee across the thigh into side control—identical finish geometry to knee-cut-pass.

After a hip switch kills a knee shield, the same slice finishes the pass—Batch 4 cluster sequencing.

Entries: toreando into HQ, force half into HQ, failed long step, hip switch clearing a shield. Counters: underhook, half recovery, invert. Read the full contingency map on knee-cut-pass; use this page for synonym search intent and cluster routing.

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Fully legal in IBJJF gi and no-gi—identical sport status to knee cut. Central in ADCC-style passing chains.

Safety

Do not drive the cutting knee into the opponent’s hip flexor with uncontrolled spikes. Keep neck safe when they underhook and bridge.

Common mistakes

  • Floating the slice without upper-body connection.
  • Treating knee slice as unrelated to knee-cut-pass (it is the same family).
  • Ignoring cluster exits (long step, toreando, smash) when the slice stalls in half.
  • Slicing into a strong reverse De La Riva without clearing the hook.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

Is knee slice the same as knee cut?

Yes in most gyms—same family. See knee-cut-pass for the full HQ system; this page is the synonym/finish accent.

Which page should I study first?

Start with knee-cut-pass for mechanics depth, then use this page for synonym routing and cluster links.

Why two entries?

Search intent splits: many people query “knee slice,” others “knee cut.” Two pages, one family, clear cross-links—no orphan.

Gi vs no-gi differences?

Shared skeleton; gi uses collar/pants, no-gi uses head pressure and underhooks—same as knee cut.

What if the slice dies in half?

Treat as half-guard passing: weave, hip switch, smash, back-step, or force half.

Knee slice vs toreando?

Slice splits through; toreando circles. Strong passers threaten both from HQ.

Pillar links?

Guard passing + half/HQ; twin link to knee-cut-pass; cluster to hip switch, force half, long step, X-pass.

The Bottom Line

Knee slice is the same splitting family as knee-cut-pass—this entry exists for “slice” search intent and Batch 4 cluster routing. Always link the twin; never treat them as unrelated orphans.
