Double Leg (Wrestling)

A wrestling double leg attacks both legs—penetrate between the feet, secure the legs, and drive or corner to the mat.

Published 12 Sep 2026 · Updated 12 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Double means both legs plus penetration plus drive.
  • Level change and pen-step are non-negotiable.
  • Corner when straight drive dies.
  • Prefixed to coexist with BJJ's page.
  • Illegal in Greco.

What Is Double Leg (Wrestling)?

The double-leg is the most recognized wrestling shot: change levels, penetrate, clasp both legs, and finish to control.

This page is Double Leg (Wrestling) to avoid overwriting BJJ double-leg; use the BJJ page for gi and no-gi grappling framing.

It depends on Wrestling Stance, Level Change, and a real Penetration Step—arm grabs without penetration are not doubles.

In Wrestling, the double is freestyle and folkstyle bread-and-butter; illegal in Greco.

Read this beside the Wrestling style hub when you need the art’s big picture; keep the focus on how Double Leg (Wrestling) behaves across freestyle, folkstyle, and Greco-Roman contexts.

How Double Leg (Wrestling) Works

Close distance with hand fighting, drop with a Level Change, and drive a Penetration Step so the lead knee splits the stance.

Arms encircle both legs or hips depending on school; head posts to the outside for many classical finishes.

Drive through, lift slightly, or turn the corner as the opponent sprawls; keep hips under shoulders.

Finish to a cover in folkstyle or continue to exposure in freestyle.

Mechanically, successful double leg (wrestling) work is usually a chain: Wrestling Stance integrity → Level Change → entry or control → finish or recovery.

If stance collapses or the Penetration Step stalls short, even textbook grips fail—too high and you meet a Sprawl (Wrestling); too loose and you invite a Whizzer or scramble.

Pair offense with the defensive layer this tool implies: shots need sprawl literacy; ties need hand-fighting; pins need Near Fall awareness in folkstyle.

Rules / Style Notes

Under United World Wrestling freestyle rules, double leg (wrestling) is judged through takedowns, exposure, and activity—not riding time.

Under NCAA/NFHS folkstyle, control, escapes, reversals, and near falls reshape how double leg (wrestling) is valued once the wrestlers hit the mat.

Greco-Roman bans holds below the waist, so leg-attack cousins of double leg (wrestling) either disappear or migrate into upper-body variants.

Confirm the rule set before importing gym habits into contest intensity—folkstyle riding does not equal freestyle par terre.

Ensure takedown control criteria under your rule set before celebrating.

Slamming finishes can be illegal—return safely in the room.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Clinic picture: explosive setup, level, penetrate, finish in one breath.

Room picture: partner sprawls; attacker re-drives the corner instead of pushing straight into hips.

Match picture: double leg (wrestling) appears when stance, ties, and timing say so, then yields to a finish, a Go-Behind, or a reset to neutral.

Room test: can you apply double leg (wrestling) after a Collar Tie or Russian Tie exchange and still recover posture? If not, chain it instead of isolating forever.

Types / Variations

Common Mistakes

  • Shooting from too far.
  • Head down, eyes on the mat.
  • Stopping on the knee under a heavy sprawl.
  • Grabbing only one leg and calling it a double.
  • Treating double leg (wrestling) as a highlight instead of a stance-and-timing skill.
  • Shooting or turning without a recovery plan when the opponent sprawls or bridges.
  • Ignoring sibling tools that set or cover the technique.

Study Notes

Coaches often describe double leg (wrestling) as “simple to show, hard to own.” The gap is usually not a missing secret—it is missing reps under hand-fighting stress, a collapsed Wrestling Stance, or a Level Change that never gets under the opponent’s hips.

When you compare rooms, you will see stylistic accents: freestyle rooms push exposure chains; folkstyle rooms push ride-and-turn; Greco rooms push upper-body locks. The encyclopedia definition of double leg (wrestling) stays stable even when accents change.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

Double vs high crotch?

Double attacks both legs; High Crotch isolates the near leg with an inside head position.

How do I beat the sprawl?

Better setups, deeper penetration, faster corner, and chain to Whizzer clears or re-shots—see Sprawl (Wrestling).

Why a wrestling-prefixed slug?

Double-Leg Takedown (BJJ) is the BJJ encyclopedia page; this entry is wrestling-specific.

Is the double used in MMA?

Widely—but cages and strikes change finishes; learn wrestling control first.

Lead knee placement?

Between the opponent's feet is the beginner checkpoint.

Can beginners start here?

Yes—after stance and level change. It teaches the whole shot engine.

The Bottom Line

Build your shot engine on the wrestling double: if your penetration and drive are real, every other takedown gets easier.
