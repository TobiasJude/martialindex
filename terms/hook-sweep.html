Hook Sweep

The hook sweep is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu reverse that uses a controlling leg hook (butterfly, outside, or lasso-fed) to off-balance and pull the top player over—clustered with butterfly and elevator under hook-based guards.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Hook + upper-body pull + hip angle = reverse.
  • Pillars: butterfly / open / sometimes lasso.
  • Cluster: butterfly sweep, elevator, tripod; top answers toreando/X/float.
  • Gi lasso hook sweeps are high percentage; no-gi prefers butterfly hooks.
  • Do not confuse with heel hooks—this is a sweep page.

What Is Hook Sweep?

"Hook sweep" is a family name: butterfly hook sweeps, open-guard hook sweeps, and lasso hook sweeps share the idea of a hooked leg controlling their base while you reverse.

Up-link butterfly, open guard, lasso. Across butterfly sweep, elevator, tripod, scissor.

How Hook Sweep Works

Insert/maintain the hook, break their posture with grips or underhooks, load the hooked side, and pull them over while you follow to top.

Top answers: clear the hook (toreando/X/knee cut), float, or kick free before the load.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Lasso and pant grips amplify hook sweeps.

No-gi. Butterfly and outside hooks with underhooks.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Butterfly hook sweep to mount; lasso hook sweep when they drive in; open-guard hook when they stand light on one leg.

Types / Variations

Butterfly hook sweep

Inside hook elevate/pull—overlaps butterfly sweep page.

Lasso hook sweep

Gi lasso feeds the hook reverse.

Outside / open-guard hook

Hook behind a standing leg and dump.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries: butterfly, lasso, open guard. Counters: clear hooks, pressure fold. Cross-link butterfly sweep when the hook is inside butterfly.

Hook Sweep vs Butterfly Sweep

Factor Hook Sweep Butterfly Sweep
Scope Family name across guards Butterfly-guard signature
Pillar options Butterfly / open / lasso Butterfly primary
Overlap High on butterfly hooks High on hook elevate
Catalog duty Broad hook reverses System butterfly reverse

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Legal. Lasso variants are gi-sport staples.

Safety

Do not twist the hooked knee into illegal entanglement territory unintentionally—keep the goal a sweep.

Common mistakes

  • Hook without upper-body control.
  • Calling every butterfly move only "hook sweep" without linking butterfly pillar.
  • Ignoring lasso when training gi.
  • No top-pass answers studied.

Related notes

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is the hook sweep?

A BJJ sweep that reverses top/bottom using the hook sweep mechanics described above.

Gi or no-gi?

Both, with different grips. Gi uses sleeves/pants/collar; no-gi uses underhooks, head control, and hooks.

Where does it sit in the catalog?

Under butterfly / open guard; across to butterfly sweep, elevator, tripod.

Good for white belts?

Often yes when drilled slowly with a clear hip path—confirm with your coach for your body type.

What if it fails?

Chain to a sibling sweep or attack (armbar/triangle/kimura) instead of forcing one path.

Hook sweep vs butterfly sweep?

Heavy overlap. See butterfly sweep for the system page.

Lasso hook sweep?

Yes in gi—see lasso guard.

The Bottom Line

Hook sweep is a family: control with a hook, reverse with hips and grips. Keep butterfly and open-guard pillars linked, and cross to butterfly/elevator/tripod siblings.
