Elevator Sweep

The elevator sweep is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu reverse that elevates the top player with hooks from closed or open guard and dumps them over - clustered with butterfly, hook, and scissor.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Hook under, elevate, pull over - follow to mount/side.
  • Pillars: closed guard and butterfly overlaps.
  • Cluster: butterfly, hook, scissor, pendulum.
  • Gi grips steer the lift; no-gi needs tight underhooks.
  • Failed elevator to triangle/armbar or sibling sweeps.

What Is Elevator Sweep?

Elevator teaching stresses lifting their hips with a hook while upper-body grips guide the dump - similar physics to butterfly elevation, often drilled from closed guard.

Across: hook, scissor, pendulum, flower.

How Elevator Sweep Works

Insert hook under a thigh, control upper body, elevate, and reverse as their base leaves the mat.

Top answers: keep weight heavy, kick the hook, posture pass, or float.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Sleeve/collar help pull through the elevate.

No-gi. Underhook + head; elevation must be decisive.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

From open/closed with a hook inserted, elevate and reverse to mount. If they sprawl, switch to butterfly system attacks.

Types / Variations

Closed/open elevator

Basic classroom elevator.

Butterfly-flavored elevator

Overlaps butterfly sweep elevation.

Elevator to triangle/armbar

When the dump is blocked, attack the posture.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries: closed to open with hook, butterfly entries. Counters: heavy hips, clear hooks.

Elevator vs Butterfly Sweep

Factor Elevator Butterfly Sweep
Teaching accent Lift like an elevator Butterfly system elevate/dump
Common start Closed/open classroom Seated butterfly
Overlap High on hook elevation High on hook elevation
Pillar Closed + butterfly links Butterfly primary

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Legal. Fundamental sweep family.

Safety

Elevate with control; avoid launching onto the neck.

Common mistakes

  • Elevating with no grips.
  • Not following up to mount.
  • Ignoring butterfly twin concepts.
  • Orphan page without closed/butterfly links.

Related notes

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is the elevator sweep?

A BJJ sweep that reverses top/bottom using the elevator sweep mechanics described above.

Gi or no-gi?

Both, with different grips. Gi uses sleeves/pants/collar; no-gi uses underhooks, head control, and hooks.

Where does it sit in the catalog?

Under closed guard / butterfly; across to butterfly, hook, scissor.

Good for white belts?

Often yes when drilled slowly with a clear hip path—confirm with your coach for your body type.

What if it fails?

Chain to a sibling sweep or attack (armbar/triangle/kimura) instead of forcing one path.

Elevator vs butterfly?

Shared elevation physics - see butterfly sweep.

From closed guard?

Yes - open, insert hook, elevate.

The Bottom Line

Elevator sweep is lift-to-reverse literacy. Tie it to closed and butterfly pillars and keep butterfly/hook/scissor in the cluster.
