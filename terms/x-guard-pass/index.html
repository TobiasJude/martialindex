X-Guard Pass

The x-guard pass answers X-guard elevation with sprawl, smash, or backstep clearing—clustered with single-leg X, butterfly to X, and X pass (speed pass family).

By Tobias Mesevage · Published 16 Sep 2026 · Updated 16 Sep 2026

Key Takeaways

  • Base first—bent knees, heavy hips.
  • Sprawl/smash when hooks are high; backstep when you can free a leg.
  • Do not stand tall in deep X.
  • Watch sweep-to-stand races.
  • Name clash: x-pass is a different speed pass.

What Is X-Guard Pass?

X-guard pass clears crossed hooks under one leg. Parent: X-guard. Related: SLX. Note: x-pass is a separate toreando-style pass name.

How It Works

Lower your base, control pants/ankles or shoulders, sprawl hips to kill elevation or smash through hooks, backstep to free a trapped leg, then pin and settle side/mount.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Pants and belt grips stabilize your base.

No-gi. Ankle rides and body locks; same lower-the-hips idea.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Deep X → you bend knees, sprawl, smash hooks flat → walk to side before they stand you up.

Types / Variations

Primary pattern

Sprawl / smash pass from X.

Competition variation

Backstep clear when one hook weakens.

Related option

Force to half then use half hub tools.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries. Vs established X/SLX. Counters. Sweeps, technical stand-ups, re-entry from butterfly.

How It Compares

Vs butterfly pass: earlier vs deeper. Vs x-pass: different technique family despite the name.

Special Considerations

Rules & sport context

Common IBJJF/ADCC; elevation races are no-gi frequent.

Safety

Sprawl without driving their knees into bad angles.

Common mistakes

  • Standing tall in deep X.
  • Confusing this page with toreando x-pass.
  • No pants/ankle control.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

How do you pass X-guard?

Lower base, sprawl/smash or backstep, clear hooks, pin hips.

Is this the same as x-pass?

No—x-pass usually means a speed/outside pass; this page is vs X-guard.

Gi or no-gi?

Both.

What next?

Side or mount; expect scramble.

Common mistake?

Standing upright in deep X.

Beginner-friendly?

Base drills yes; live elevation is hard.

Martial Index home?

Under X-guard next to SLX and butterfly transitions.

The Bottom Line

X-guard pass is survival under elevation—lower your hips, then smash or backstep. Do not confuse with x-pass.
