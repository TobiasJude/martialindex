Double Under Pass

The double under pass is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu guard pass that puts both arms under the opponent's legs, stacks their hips, and clears to side control—famously chaining into crucifix attacks when an arm is trapped.

By Tobias Mesevage · Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Both arms under the legs + stack + clear.
  • High control; watch for triangles if you posture poorly early.
  • Gi grips on pants help; no-gi uses clasps behind the back/hips.
  • Can feed crucifix when an arm is left behind.
  • Related: stack pass, pressure passing.

What Is Double Under Pass?

The double under pass threads both of your arms under the bottom player's thighs/knees, clasps or grips behind them, and stacks their hips toward their head so you can walk or circle past the legs into a pin. Marcelo Garcia popularized crisp double-under-to-crucifix chains. It sits inside the broader guard passing family.

How Double Under Pass Works

From inside open or closed guard residues, shoot both underhooks under the legs, lift and stack, keep their shoulders threatened, and clear a side. If an arm is trapped under a leg, transition toward crucifix.

Bottom player's job. Prevent both unders, threaten triangles/omoplatas early, and shrimp hips down before the stack peaks.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Pants grips amplify the stack and control.

No-gi. Clasp behind the hips/back and drive with shoulders.

MMA context. Stack carefully—neck safety and strike awareness matter.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Alex shoots double unders from Jordan's open guard, stacks, clears to the side, and when Jordan's arm is trapped, attacks the crucifix.

Gi. Pants grips freeze the legs during the stack.

No-gi. Alex clasps and drives without cloth.

Types / Variations

Classic double under

Both arms under, stack, clear to side control.

To crucifix

Trap an arm and rotate into crucifix finishes.

To side control / north-south

Standard pin finishes without crucifix.

Defensive notes for bottom

Hip down, triangle threats, and peeling one underhook early.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries. From combat base; after stuffing butterfly lifts; from failed toreando when legs elevate.

Counters. Triangle/omoplata races, peeling an underhook, forcing posture.

Failure mode. Soft stack with head in triangle danger.

Double Under vs Stack Pass

Factor Double Under Stack
Arm position Both under the legs Varies—often over/around while stacking
Crucifix path Very natural Less automatic
Feel Underhook stack General fold toward the head
Risk Triangle if loose Neck pressure if cranked

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Legal. Pass scores when stabilized. Crucifix submissions legal per standard rules.

Safety

Do not crank cervical spines in the stack. Tap early to triangles if you are on bottom of a failed double under.

Common mistakes

  • Head posture inviting triangles.
  • Stacking without clearing a side.
  • Missing the crucifix when an arm is gifted.
  • In the gi: forgetting pants grips.
  • Dumping partners on their necks.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is the double under pass?

A pass with both arms under the legs that stacks and clears to a pin—often chaining to crucifix.

Double under vs stack pass?

Double under specifies both arms under; stack is the broader fold-toward-the-head family.

How do you hit crucifix from double under?

When an arm is trapped under a leg during the clear, rotate into crucifix control and finish.

How do you defend double unders?

Prevent both underhooks, threaten triangle, and get your hips down before the stack peaks.

Does it work in no-gi?

Yes—clasp behind the hips and drive; pants grips are simply unavailable.

Is double under a pressure pass?

Yes—it lives in the pressure/stack family rather than upright speed passing.

Why do I get triangled when I shoot double unders?

Your head and posture are in the pocket without controlling their hips—stack with safer head position or clear faster.

The Bottom Line

The double under pass stacks with both arms under the legs and clears to a pin—watch for crucifix gifts and triangle danger. See stack pass and crucifix.
