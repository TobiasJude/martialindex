Lockdown Sweep

The lockdown sweep is the offensive umbrella from lockdown half guard—especially the electric chair sweep and whip entries—clustered with 10th Planet, half guard, and half-guard passes.

By Tobias Mesevage · Published 16 Sep 2026 · Updated 16 Sep 2026

Key Takeaways

  • Lockdown triangulates their leg; sweeps use the whip to off-balance.
  • Electric chair is the flagship named finish/sweep.
  • No-gi native (10th Planet) but usable in gi with adjustments.
  • Top answers: free the trapped leg early, stand, or smash the free side.
  • Umbrella page—detail live on electric-chair-sweep.

What Is Lockdown Sweep?

Lockdown sweep covers reverses launched once your legs are triangulated around one of theirs in half guard. Parent: lockdown. Flagship child: electric chair sweep. Style home: 10th Planet.

How It Works

Establish lockdown, win underhooks, whip the entangled leg to flatten or elevate them, then finish electric chair as sweep and/or stretch, or come up into truck/back paths.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Works; pants grips can assist the free leg, but the system is no-gi famous.

No-gi. Native environment—skin friction and underhooks matter.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

They smash half → you lockdown → whip to electric chair → sweep to top or submit the stretch.

Types / Variations

Primary pattern

Electric chair sweep (primary).

Competition variation

Whip to dogfight / wrestle-up without full electric chair.

Related option

Truck / back exposures when they roll from the stretch.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries. From flat half when you can triangle the leg. Counters. Extract the foot before the triangle sets; pressure the free hip; see half guard pass hub.

How It Compares

This page is the umbrella; electric-chair-sweep is the deep technique note. Vs knee-shield: different entanglement.

Special Considerations

Rules & sport context

Legal in most adult IBJJF/ADCC contexts; groin stretches require training care—verify event rules (IBJJF, ADCC).

Safety

Electric chair can stress hips/groin—tap early; drill progressively.

Common mistakes

  • Loose lockdown that they step out of.
  • Whipping without underhooks.
  • Cranking the stretch as ego instead of positional sweep.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is a lockdown sweep?

Any reverse from triangulated-leg lockdown half—especially electric chair and whip entries.

Is electric chair the only lockdown sweep?

It is the flagship; whip-ups and truck paths also count.

Does it work in the gi?

Yes with adjustments; the system is no-gi famous.

What should you chain next?

Top pressure, truck, or back—see electric-chair-sweep.

Common mistake?

Loose triangle or whipping without underhooks.

Is it beginner-friendly?

Lockdown control first; electric chair is intermediate with safety coaching.

Where does it fit on Martial Index?

Under lockdown, linking to electric-chair-sweep and 10th Planet.

The Bottom Line

Lockdown sweep is the umbrella—build sticky lockdown, then specialize via electric chair rather than collecting random tricks.
