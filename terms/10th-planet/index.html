10th Planet Jiu-Jitsu

10th Planet Jiu-Jitsu is Eddie Bravo’s no-gi-oriented system and affiliate network, famous for rubber-guard concepts, lockdown-style half guard, and a distinct training culture. Cross-link open guard, Gracie legacy context, and sport/no-gi competition paths.

Published 12 Sep 2026 · Updated 12 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • No-gi identity is central.
  • Named positional systems (rubber guard, lockdown) are calling cards—not the whole of BJJ.
  • Affiliate culture varies.
  • Innovation story sits inside wider no-gi evolution.
  • Encyclopedia ≠ merch.

What Is 10th Planet Jiu-Jitsu?

A brand and curriculum family emphasizing no-gi grips, interlocking guards, and playful naming.

Grew in visibility with MMA/pop-culture crossovers and instructional media.

One influential branch among many modern no-gi approaches.

How 10th Planet Jiu-Jitsu Works

Learn concepts from reputable instructors; don’t rely on meme versions.

Integrate rubber-guard ideas with fundamentals—posture, frames, escapes.

If your gym is not 10th Planet, still study ideas as modular tools.

Compete under the rules you train for (IBJJF no-gi vs submission-only differ).

Respect tapping culture in entanglements.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. 10th Planet identity is primarily no-gi; gi training elsewhere still builds attributes.

No-gi. Home ruleset—sweat, underhooks, and lockdown mechanics.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Eddie Bravo explainers define the brand’s no-gi ethos for newcomers.

Half-guard lockdown sequences show systemized bottom control—pair with standard half-guard literacy.

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Some 10th Planet athletes compete under IBJJF no-gi; others favor different promotions. Rubber-guard habits must respect each ruleset’s illegalities.

Safety

Entangled guards and leg entries need clear tapping culture. Newer students should learn escapes before deep lockdowns.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping basics for Instagram rubber guard.
  • Ignoring wrestling.
  • Mocking gi training as useless.
  • Assuming every no-gi gym is 10th Planet.
  • Promotional “join HQ” readings of encyclopedia text.

Related notes

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

Is 10th Planet only rubber guard?

Rubber guard is famous, but curricula include broader no-gi material.

Do I need to join to learn the ideas?

Concepts circulate widely; affiliates teach branded progressions.

Gi practitioners—worth studying?

Yes, selectively—for no-gi attributes and entanglement awareness.

The Bottom Line

10th Planet is a influential no-gi system brand—study its ideas as part of modern BJJ’s diversity, with the same etiquette and fundamentals any serious room needs.
