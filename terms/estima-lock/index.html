Estima Lock

The Estima lock (sock lock) is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu foot lock that traps a posted foot against your torso and finishes with toe-hold-like pressure—famous against open-guard side-kick frames, with IBJJF treating it carefully among foot locks.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Let the foot come to you—do not chase.
  • Trap foot to hip/torso, then figure-four arms.
  • Compress/turn; do not lift the foot out.
  • Often categorized with toe-hold legality—verify.
  • Links to RDLR, open guard, and toe hold.

What Is Estima Lock?

Developed/popularized by the Estima brothers, the Estima lock catches a foot planted on your belly/chest (common in modern open guard) by wrapping the ankle and finishing with sharp toe-hold pressure while your hip blocks escape. It can appear while you remain more upright than classic ashi finishes.

Related: toe hold, straight ankle lock, open-guard frames.

How Estima Lock Works

When they side-kick your midsection, trap the foot against your hip, slide a forearm to the ankle, figure-four your arms, step/turn your hip into the top of the foot, and compress with a toe-hold vector rather than lifting.

If the foot pops, you chased or lifted—reset frames instead of ripping.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Same trap; pants can help secondary control.

No-Gi. Sticky hips and timing matter more.

Referees sometimes confuse aggressive Estima finishes with heel-hook categories—know your rule set and referee tendencies, but always defer to written rules.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Jordan plants a foot on Alex’s belly from open guard. Alex wraps the ankle, turns the hip in, and finishes the Estima as Jordan tries to retract.

Types / Variations

Open-guard belly plant

Classic trigger.

RDLR / modern guard

When hooks put feet on the torso.

Pass-counter timing

As you pressure pass, they post the foot into the trap.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries: open-guard foot posts, RDLR frames, standing pass attempts.

Counters: do not park the foot on their belly; retract early; hide toes.

Escapes: extract before the figure-four closes—tap fast once trapped.

Estima Lock vs Toe Hold

Factor Estima Lock Toe Hold
Trigger Foot on torso Ashi/entanglement foot
Posture Often more upright Often seated ashi
Feel Sock-lock compression Kimura foot twist
Rule vibe Toe-hold family discussions Toe-hold family

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Often discussed as a toe-hold category attack for IBJJF purposes—verify the current IBJJF rulebook for belt legality. Anecdotes about referee confusion with heel hooks exist; compete conservatively if unclear.

Safety

Comes on very fast. Drill recognition more than hard finishes. Immediate release.

Common mistakes

  • Chasing the foot.
  • Lifting instead of compressing.
  • Ignoring belt legality.
  • Leaving hip unblocked so the foot pops.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is an Estima lock?

A foot lock trapping a posted foot to your torso with toe-hold pressure.

Who created it?

Associated with Victor and Braulio Estima.

Legal?

Usually discussed with toe-hold rules—verify current IBJJF.

Main tip?

Let the foot come to you; compress, don’t lift.

Gi or no-gi?

Both.

Defense?

Do not park your foot on their belly.

Related to heel hook?

Different mechanism; referees may still scrutinize—know the book.

The Bottom Line

Estima lock is the sock-lock trap of open guard: invite the foot, wrap, hip in, compress. Cross-link toe hold and verify legality.
