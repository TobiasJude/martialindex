What Is Balloon Sweep?

Balloon Sweep is a closed-guard sweep that lifts the opponent vertically with the legs—like inflating a balloon—then guides them over into mount. In gym language you may also hear balloon / elevating closed-guard sweep. Train it as a named pattern with clear entries and exits—not a vague vibe.

How It Works

Break posture, open the guard, place feet on hips (or insert a butterfly hook), pull upper body in, then explosively extend the hips to elevate and roll them into mount. Control beats speed: establish the grips or frames that make the next decision obvious.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi vs no-gi notes. Collar and sleeve grips steer the lift; no-gi uses neck/wrist control and tighter hooks.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

In live training, balloon sweep usually appears as a short window: you recognize the grips/angles, commit the mechanics above, and either finish the position change or chain to a sibling technique when they defend.

Types / Variations

Primary pattern

Standard balloon sweep as described in the hero video and fundamental classes.

Competition variation

Competition variation: tighter grips, earlier chains, less telegraphing.

Related option

Related option: transition into a sibling from the Related notes when the primary finish stalls.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries come from the parent position(s) (Closed Guard). Counters include framing early, removing the key grip, and forcing a different cluster (for example recovering guard or changing the pin). Escapes prioritize creating space before the structure fully sets.

How It Compares

More vertical than Scissor Sweep or Hip Bump Sweep; cousins Elevator Sweep mechanics.

Special Considerations

Rules & sport context

Generally legal in BJJ; check Ibjjf Rules and Adcc Rules for crank/reaping/slam nuances when relevant to this pattern (especially neck and spinal pressure).

Safety

Communicate, tap early, and avoid ballistic force on the neck and shoulders. This page is reference—not live coaching.

Common mistakes

Rushing without grips; stalling without a next move; confusing balloon sweep with a nearby sibling name.
