Short Choke

The short choke is a compact rear strangle from back control: the wrap is shallower than a textbook figure-four RNC, the forearm cuts across the neck, and you often clasp palm-to-palm or gable with the support elbow driving into their back. It is the usual answer to a tucked chin.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • “Short” means less arm threaded around the neck—not a weaker idea.
  • Different from palm-to-palm RNC, which can still be a deep wrap with a clasp finish.
  • Jeremy Horn–style teaching: when they strip the top arm, convert rather than restart.
  • Can be more windpipe-involved—tap early and train under a coach.
  • Setup still needs a back: RNC setup from back.

What Is Short Choke?

When the chin is down or your arm is not deep enough for a figure-four, you still have a rear choke. The short choke uses a shorter path—often the forearm across the neck with a compact clasp—and a support elbow that shelves on their back to remove slack.

Gyms disagree on naming. Some call any clasp a short choke; others reserve “short” for the shallow wrap and keep palm-to-palm for a deep RNC with a clasp. Martial Index uses the second convention so the three pages do not clone each other.

It is older than the internet meme: catch wrestling and early MMA used shallow rear chokes constantly. The modern BJJ job is to treat it as a conversion, not a sloppy RNC.

How Short Choke Works

From a seatbelt, get some forearm on the neck even if you cannot park the elbow under the chin. Clasp palm-to-palm or grab your own wrist. Walk the support elbow around so it presses their upper back.

Push the elbow into the back as you pull the choking forearm across. Head pressure helps. This is a shorter vise than the figure-four.

If depth suddenly appears, convert to a figure-four. If they peel the top arm, keep the short choke instead of resetting to a losing hand fight.

Hooks still matter. A short choke without lower-body control is just a temporary hug.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. You may prefer a collar if the short choke is messy—but the naked short choke still works in the gi.

No-gi. The conversion you need when chins stay down.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Alex cannot clear the chin, shelves an elbow on the back, and finishes a short choke from the same seatbelt.

Jordan’s RNC top arm gets stripped; they convert to the short choke instead of giving up the back.

Types / Variations

RNC-to-short conversion

They defend the deep wrap; you shorten the grip.

Under-arm short choke

Some systems send the underhook arm across the throat—different path, same “short” idea.

Gable + elbow on the back

The Horn / no-gi picture most videos show.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Triggered by a defended figure-four or a shallow chin find. Counters: two-on-one the forearm, chin down, slide off the hooks. Escapes: peel before the elbow shelves on the back.

Short Choke vs Figure-Four RNC

Factor Short choke Figure-four RNC
Depth Shallow wrap Deep elbow-under-chin
Grip Clasp / wrist grab + back elbow Bicep + hand behind head
When Chin tucked / arm stripped Chin window won
Comfort Often harsher on the throat Cleaner blood choke

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Adult rear chokes are standard legal chokes. Always verify the current IBJJF and ADCC (or event) rulebooks for division, belt, age, and gi vs no-gi. Martial Index gives orientation—not invented illegal-move lists.

Safety

Short chokes can be ugly on the windpipe. Apply gradually, tap early, and never crank the head to “make it work.”

Common mistakes

  • Calling every failed RNC a short choke without changing the elbow line.
  • No back-shelf—just a loose gable in space.
  • Abandoning hooks while you adjust the clasp.
  • Ripping the throat instead of converting or resetting.

Related notes

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

Is short choke just a bad RNC?

No. It is a different depth and elbow line, used when the deep RNC is blocked.

Palm-to-palm or short?

Palm-to-palm can still be deep. Short is the shallow wrap. They can share a clasp.

Jeremy Horn version?

A common video reference for converting a stripped RNC into a short choke.

Safe to drill?

Yes, slowly. It can hit the throat harder than a figure-four.

Gi alternative?

Collar chokes if the short choke is messy.

Need hooks?

Yes—same back-control rules.

Legal?

Standard adult choke—verify your current book.

The Bottom Line

Short choke is the shallow rear option: forearm on the neck, clasp, elbow on the back. Use it when figure-four depth is gone, not as a sloppy substitute for a real setup.
