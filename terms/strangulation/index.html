Strangulation

Strangulation is the precise term for restricting blood flow to the brain via the neck vessels. In BJJ, most “chokes” that finish cleanly are strangles. See blood choke (gym synonym) and contrast air choke.

By Tobias Mesevage · Published 16 Sep 2026 · Updated 16 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Technical meaning: vascular restriction—sister page blood choke.
  • Everyday BJJ still says “choke” for RNC, triangle, lapel attacks.
  • Contrast air choke (airway). Prefer clean strangle mechanics in sport.
  • Medical caution: neck submissions can cause unconsciousness or airway injury. In training, tap early; partners release immediately on tap or loss of consciousness and seek qualified medical help if needed. This page is reference—not medical advice.
  • Hub: chokes.

What Is Strangulation?

Strangulation names the mechanism: pressure on the sides of the neck affecting cerebral circulation. Catalog neighbors: blood choke, air choke, chokes.

How It Works

Arms, legs, or lapels compress the carotids (and sometimes jugulars) while controlling the head. Neurological effects can appear quickly. Training culture: tap early; never hold after unconsciousness.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Collar strangles are fabric-assisted strangulations when the blade tracks the vessels.

No-gi. RNC and many triangles are textbook strangulations.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Instructors who say “strangle, not choke” are emphasizing vascular mechanics over airway crushing.

Types / Variations

Primary pattern

Arm strangulations (RNC family).

Competition variation

Leg strangulations (triangle family).

Related option

Lapel strangulations (collar family).

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Defense literacy mirrors the named submissions: RNC defense, triangle defense.

How It Compares

Vs blood choke: largely synonymous; “strangle” is the cleaner technical word. Vs air choke: vessels vs airway.

Special Considerations

Rules & sport context

Sport BJJ allows strangulation submissions within rulebooks; medical stops apply.

Safety

Medical caution: neck submissions can cause unconsciousness or airway injury. In training, tap early; partners release immediately on tap or loss of consciousness and seek qualified medical help if needed. This page is reference—not medical advice.

Common mistakes

  • Using the word debate to ignore safety.
  • Holding after blackout.
  • Equating all neck pressure with safe sport strangulation.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What does strangulation mean in BJJ?

Vascular restriction at the neck—technically what many “blood chokes” are.

Why do people still say choke?

Habit and naming tradition (RNC, triangle choke)—meaning is usually a strangle.

Strangle vs air choke?

Strangle ≈ vessels; air choke ≈ airway.

Is blood choke the same?

In gym use, yes—blood choke is the common synonym.

Safety?

Tap early; release on tap/blackout; seek medical help if needed.

Examples?

RNC, triangles, and many collar strangles.

Related notes?

Blood choke, air choke, chokes, RNC, triangle, lapel choke.

The Bottom Line

Strangulation is the accurate mechanism word—train clean vascular pressure, keep air-choke cranks out of ego rolls, and honor every tap.
