What Is Knee Reap Rules?

A knee reap (gym language) is a configuration that can load the knee across the midline. Exact legal definitions live in federation PDFs and change—do not memorize a blog list.

Martial Index covers the mechanical idea on reaping. This page exists for searchers who need the right next clicks: official books + related concepts.

Because belt, age, gi vs no-gi, and year matter, any static third-party “illegal until X” table can be wrong the week after it is published.

How Knee Reap Rules Works

Practical workflow: identify your event → open the current PDF → find foul/legal sections for your division → ask the rules meeting if unclear. Gym lore is not a source of truth.

Training implication: if your gym forbids reaping at your rank, train entanglements for sweeps and legal finishes only.

Related positions—saddle, SLX, 50/50—can be legal to hold while a specific pressure or finish is not. Separate position from foul action.

Cross-check heel hook the same way: entries and finishes are not automatically paired in every rule set.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi IBJJF-style events. Expect tighter historical language around reaping; still verify the current book for your belt and age.

No-gi / ADCC-style events. Often freer adult leg games—still read the event PDF and any extras.

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Verify the current rulebook. Martial Index will not invent illegal lists, belt charts, or point tables for reaping. Open IBJJF Rules and ADCC Rules Overview.

Safety

Reaping configurations can injure knees even when legal. Slow training, informed partners, and instant releases beat highlight culture.

Common mistakes

  • Quoting an outdated blog illegal list.
  • Assuming gi and no-gi match.
  • Assuming every belt matches adult black-belt no-gi culture.
  • Skipping the rules meeting.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What are knee reap rules?

Federation and event rules about when a reaping-style knee configuration is legal or a foul—always read the current PDF.

Does Martial Index list what is illegal?

No. Lists go stale. We link concepts and official rule pages instead.

Are IBJJF and ADCC the same?

No. Orient with both pages, then verify the event you entered.

Is the saddle always illegal?

Do not assume. Position vs foul action vs finish are separate questions in the book.

Where do I start as a white belt?

Ask your coach what your gym allows; open the PDF before any competition.

How does this relate to heel hooks?

Heel hooks are finishes; reaping is a configuration/foul topic—linked but not identical.

What if my gym and my event disagree?

Train to the stricter standard in the gym; compete to the event PDF.
