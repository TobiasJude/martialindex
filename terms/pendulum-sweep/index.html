Pendulum Sweep

The pendulum sweep is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu closed-guard reverse that swings your hips like a pendulum under/around the top player’s base, pulling them over—often chained with armbars and the flower sweep.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Swing under the base with strong upper-body grips; come up past their posts.
  • Pillar: closed guard. Cluster: flower, scissor, hip bump, elevator.
  • Famous armbar–pendulum chain when they post to stop the sweep.
  • Gi sleeve/pant grips shine; no-gi uses underhooks and wrist control.
  • Link across Batch 4 closed-guard sweeps—no orphans.

What Is Pendulum Sweep?

Pendulum (sometimes taught near flower/Xande-style swings) uses a hip pendulum under their weight while grips pull them across your body into a reverse. It is closed-guard bread-and-butter next to scissor and flower.

Up-link closed guard; across flower, scissor, hip bump, armbar.

How Pendulum Sweep Works

Control a sleeve and pant (or underhook), open guard, swing the far leg and hips under their center, and pull them over the pendulum path into mount or side.

Top answers. Post, posture, extract the arm, or stand into passing. Expect the armbar when you post incorrectly.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Sleeve + pant is classic. Collar assists posture breaks.

No-gi. Underhook + wrist/head control; smaller margin for error.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Closed guard → grip fight → pendulum swing → mount. If they post the arm, transition to armbar without releasing control.

If the swing dies, switch to scissor or flower using the same grips.

Types / Variations

Classic pendulum to mount

Hip swing under base with sleeve/pant pull.

Pendulum–armbar chain

When they post, abandon sweep path for the armbar.

Xande / flower-adjacent swing

Overlap with flower teaching—see flower entry for pant-heavy variants.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries: broken posture closed guard, failed scissor, collar-sleeve control. Counters: posture, base, extract grips. Chain within the closed-guard sweep cluster.

Pendulum vs Flower Sweep

Factor Pendulum Flower
Accent Hip pendulum under base Often pant/sleeve “flower” pull
Overlap High—some gyms merge names High—some gyms merge names
Pillar Closed guard Closed guard
Attack chain Armbar common Armbar / omoplata common

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Legal everywhere in IBJJF. Fundamental sport sweep.

Safety

Control their head/shoulder path so you do not dump them onto a bent neck. Drill with cooperative tempo first.

Common mistakes

  • No upper-body grips.
  • Tiny swing that does not load their weight.
  • Forcing pendulum when flower/scissor is open.
  • Forgetting the armbar branch.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is the pendulum sweep?

A BJJ sweep that reverses top/bottom using the pendulum sweep mechanics described above.

Gi or no-gi?

Both, with different grips. Gi uses sleeves/pants/collar; no-gi uses underhooks, head control, and hooks.

Where does it sit in the catalog?

Under closed guard; across to flower, scissor, hip bump.

Good for white belts?

Often yes when drilled slowly with a clear hip path—confirm with your coach for your body type.

What if it fails?

Chain to a sibling sweep or attack (armbar/triangle/kimura) instead of forcing one path.

Is pendulum the same as flower?

Closely related; some schools merge names. See flower sweep with cross-links.

Pendulum to armbar?

Yes—when they post to stop the sweep, the armbar is often there.

The Bottom Line

Pendulum sweep is closed-guard swinging power. Keep it tied to closed guard and the flower/scissor/hip-bump cluster—and always train the armbar branch.
