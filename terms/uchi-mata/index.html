Uchi Mata

Uchi mata is judo’s inner-thigh throw: off-balance forward, turn in, and reap the inside thigh to rotate them over—adapted to BJJ with grip and no-gi variants, clustered under takedown beside osoto gari.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Kuzushi forward before the reap.
  • Hips in; head and hands finish the rotation.
  • Chains with osoto and ouchi.
  • Gi grips help; no-gi needs underhooks/head.
  • Land ready to pass—not admiring the throw.

What Is Uchi Mata?

Classic judo uchi mata loads uke forward, enters with a turning step, and reaps the inner thigh while pulling. BJJ versions shorten entries for shorter exchanges and often chain from failed osoto or Russian ties (as modern judo-for-BJJ systems show).

Prefer archive judo photos for study pages—do not rely on banned uchi-mata* stock stems in this catalog’s media policy.

Sport: a clean uchi mata that stabilizes top can score as a takedown under IBJJF-like systems—verify. ADCC rewards the control that follows.

How Uchi Mata Works

Pull forward off-balance, step and turn hips in, contact inner thigh, reap while hands/head guide the fall, follow to top.

If they step out, pivot to osoto or foot sweeps.

Drill uchikomi (entry reps) before full throws.

No-gi: secure underhook/head control first.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Classic lapel/sleeve kumikata.

No-Gi. Underhook, Russian tie, or head-and-arm entries.

Both: do not reap without kuzushi—injury city.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Alex pulls Jordan onto the toes, turns in, reaps uchi mata, and kneels into a pass.

Failed osoto loads the inside; Alex switches to uchi mata.

No-gi: Alex’s underhook entry hits a shorter inner-thigh dump.

Types / Variations

Classic gi uchi mata

Lapel/sleeve, full turn-in.

No-gi adaptations

Underhook / Russian-tie entries.

Osoto–uchi chains

Direction changes off defense.

Compact BJJ entries

Shorter turns for sport timing.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries: forward pressure, failed outside reaps, snap-downs that reload forward.

Counters: step over the reap, sprawl hips, and clog the turn with hands.

Failed throw: do not stay bent—reset grips or pull.

Uchi Mata vs Osoto Gari

Factor Uchi Mata Osoto Gari
Reap line Inside thigh Outside / back of leg
Typical kuzushi Forward Backward / sideways
Chain partner Often osoto Often uchi / sasae

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Throw scoring is takedown scoring in BJJ sport—control after the fall matters. Confirm current IBJJF criteria. Illegal variants (dangerous drops on the head/neck) are never acceptable training.

Safety

Uchi mata injures knees and necks when forced. Progressive uchikomi, qualified coaching, and clean breakfalls required.

Common mistakes

  • Reaping without forward load.
  • Head diving.
  • No follow-up pass.
  • Fighting their strength square.

The Bottom Line

Uchi mata is forward judo power in a BJJ handshake. Load them, turn in, reap clean, and pass before they lock closed guard.
