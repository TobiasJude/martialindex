Banana Split

The banana split is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu stretch submission that pries the legs apart—most famously from the truck—attacking adductors/hips, with chains into calf slicer, crotch ripper, and twister paths.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Truck control first; stretch second.
  • S-grip / gable on the far leg while extending your own.
  • Flexible partners may not tap—have backups.
  • Link crab-ride and twister-side-control.
  • Apply slowly; adductor strains heal slowly.

What Is Banana Split?

From truck (or lockdown pathways into truck), you control one leg with a figure-four and pull the far leg to create a forced split. It is a flexibility/pain submission more than a neat joint snap—and a gateway into calf slicers and twisters when the split stalls.

See truck, crab ride, twister side control.

How Banana Split Works

Establish truck. Reach the far ankle/leg with a strong grip, sit back appropriately, and extend your trapped-leg structure while pulling the far leg toward you.

If they are too flexible, transition to calf slicer, crotch-ripper style hip attacks, or climb toward twister/back.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Pant grips help the far-leg pull.

No-Gi. Classic 10th Planet truck culture.

Rule sets vary on stretch submissions and twister follow-ups—verify.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Alex inserts truck from a failed back take, grabs Jordan’s far ankle, and forces the banana split until Jordan taps—or switches to calf slicer when Jordan’s flexibility eats the stretch.

Types / Variations

Truck banana split

Primary model.

Lockdown to truck

Electric-chair adjacent pathways.

Crotch-ripper cousin

Different hip vector when one leg extends and the other flexes.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries: truck from back, lockdown lifts, turtle rides.

Counters: keep legs together, deny truck insertion, turn into the rider.

Escapes: close the legs and invert carefully—tap if adductors load.

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Twister / spinal-twist finishes and some truck-family attacks are heavily restricted or illegal under many IBJJF-style rule sets. Verify current IBJJF vs ADCC (or promoter) rules before competing. Train spine torque only under qualified coaches. Banana-split style stretches may be treated differently than twisters—still verify the event packet.

Safety

Adductor and hip injuries. Slow stretch only. Never bounce.

Common mistakes

  • Stretching without truck control.
  • No backup when they are flexible.
  • Ripping the split.
  • Ignoring calf-slicer/twister branches.

Related notes

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is a banana split?

A stretch forcing the legs apart, usually from truck.

Same as crotch ripper?

Related truck stretches with different hip vectors.

Does flexibility kill it?

Often—have calf slicer/twister backups.

Gi or no-gi?

Both; big in no-gi truck games.

Legal?

Verify IBJJF/ADCC/event rules for stretches and follow-ups.

Entry?

Truck from back or lockdown.

Safety?

Slow only; adductor strains linger.

The Bottom Line

Banana split is truck’s stretch: control, pry, and switch when flexibility wins. Keep calf slicer and twister ready.
