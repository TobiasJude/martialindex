Float Pass

The float pass is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu approach that rides the bottom player’s elevation—especially from butterfly—and redirects it to clear past the hooks into a pin, sitting in the speed / connection cluster under guard passing.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Float with them, then land past the hooks—do not freeze mid-float.
  • Pillars: butterfly / open guard + guard passing.
  • Cluster: X-pass, toreando, folding, jumping, body lock.
  • Gi grips help steer the float; no-gi needs wrist control and hip timing.
  • Failed float → underhook and sweep; have pressure exits ready.

What Is Float Pass?

Float passing means you do not immediately smash: you stay mobile on top of butterfly or open-guard elevation, ride the hop, and step or circle past when their hooks disconnect. Jeff Glover–style “floating” and modern butterfly-pass variations share this idea.

Catalog links: up to guard passing and butterfly; across to X-pass, toreando, folding (pressure opposite), and athletic clears like jumping.

How Float Pass Works

Accept temporary elevation, keep base wide enough not to be swept, wait for hooks to lighten, then clear a hip and land with chest connection.

Bottom player’s job. Keep hooks heavy, off-balance during the float, elevate into a sweep, or recover closed/seated before the clear.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Sleeve and pant grips steer their posture while you float. Collar frames from bottom try to freeze you.

No-gi. Timing and underhook denial matter more; floating without hand fighting is how butterfly sweeps land.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Against butterfly, the passer rides a hop, waits for one hook to lighten, and steps past into side control—or collapses into a fold if elevation dies.

Same open-guard cluster as X-pass/toreando: float when they elevate; circle when they extend feet.

Types / Variations

Butterfly float pass

Ride hooks, clear when one disconnects.

Float into HQ / knee slice

Land in headquarters and finish with the cut family.

Float then fold

If they stop elevating, switch to pressure fold/smash.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries: butterfly elevation, seated open guard hops, failed smash that turns into a scramble. Counters: classic butterfly sweeps, underhooks, taking the back. Exit to pressure cluster (folding, smash) when floating stops being safe.

Float Pass vs Folding Pass

Factor Float Pass Folding Pass
Feel Ride elevation, then clear Collapse legs under chest
Best vs Active butterfly hops Static butterfly / packaged legs
Pillar Speed / connection passing Pressure passing
Cluster mates X-pass, toreando, jumping Smash, stack, over-under

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Legal. Floating without progress can look like stalling in some no-gi events—clear or switch to pressure.

Safety

Keep base; do not float so high you land on your head. Control landings when clearing past elevated hips.

Common mistakes

  • Floating with narrow base—getting butterfly-swept.
  • No plan when elevation stops (fold/smash exit).
  • Ignoring hand fighting in no-gi.
  • Orphaning the page from butterfly and guard-passing pillars.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is a float pass?

A pass that rides the bottom player’s elevation—especially butterfly—and clears past when hooks lighten.

Float vs smash?

Float rides movement; smash/fold kills space. Strong passers switch between them.

Best against butterfly?

Yes as a primary context—also useful vs seated open guard. See butterfly guard.

Gi or no-gi?

Both. Gi grips help steer; no-gi needs sharper timing and underhook denial.

Why do I get swept floating?

Base too narrow or hands late. Widen base, fight underhooks, or fold earlier.

Related sweeps from bottom?

Butterfly sweep and hook sweep punish bad floats—study both sides of the cluster.

Pillar–cluster links?

Up: butterfly, open guard, guard passing. Across: X-pass, toreando, folding, jumping.

The Bottom Line

Float pass is mobile top play against elevation: ride, clear, land. Keep it linked to butterfly and guard passing, and keep folding/smash as the pressure exit when floating dies.
