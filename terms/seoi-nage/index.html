Seoi Nage

Seoi nage is judo’s shoulder throw: turn in, load them over the back/shoulder, and rotate them to the mat—including drop-seoi variants common in BJJ, clustered with osoto gari under takedown.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Elbow control is the slot for the shoulder.
  • Turn fully; leave no daylight.
  • Drop seoi fits BJJ timing.
  • Stuff defense often opens osoto.
  • Follow through to a passable landing.

What Is Seoi Nage?

Ippon seoi traps an arm, turns the back into their chest, and throws over the shoulder. Morote and other grips vary the hand story. Drop seoi kneels to beat taller or sprawling opponents—frequently seen in IBJJF highlight throws.

BJJ adaptation prioritizes safe landings and immediate pass posture over Olympic amplitude. See grip fighting.

Scoring is takedown scoring when control criteria are met—verify current books. ADCC sees fewer pure seoi without gi handles, but drop variants still appear.

How Seoi Nage Works

Secure the arm, step and turn hips in, load the back under their chest, rotate and drop them, keep a grip for follow-up.

Drop variant: same slot, knee(s) to mat to create the slope.

If stuffed, pivot to osoto or foot sweeps.

Uchikomi and controlled nagekomi before live.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Natural home—sleeve/lapel seoi.

No-Gi. Harder; needs overhook/arm wrap entries and lower amplitude.

Both: never throw someone on their head/neck.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Alex wins a sleeve, turns in ippon seoi, and lands in side control.

Drop seoi against a taller opponent puts Alex on a knee and finishes the rotation.

Stuffed seoi becomes osoto when Jordan posts tall.

Types / Variations

Ippon seoi nage

Single-arm shoulder throw.

Morote seoi

Two-hand lapel variations.

Drop seoi

Kneeling entry popular in BJJ.

Seoi–osoto chain

When the turn is stuffed.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries: sleeve dominance, reaction to their forward pressure, feints.

Counters: sprawl hips back, clog the elbow slot, and circle behind.

Failed: do not stay kneeling under them—scramble to guard or stand.

Seoi Nage vs Uchi Mata

Factor Seoi Nage Uchi Mata
Load Shoulder / back Inner-thigh reap
Turn Back faces them Side/hip entry
Drop option Very common Less central
Stuff answer Often osoto Often osoto / foot sweep

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

High-level overview: successful seoi with stabilized top control may earn takedown points under IBJJF-like scoring—always verify the live rulebook. Dangerous landings can be penalized and are unsafe regardless.

Safety

Shoulder and elbow injuries happen when partners do not roll cleanly. Coach supervision; progressive speed; no competition throws on unprepared partners.

Common mistakes

  • Turning without the elbow slot.
  • Throwing onto the head/neck.
  • Dropping and freezing under their weight.
  • No chain when stuffed.

The Bottom Line

Seoi nage loads the shoulder slot and rotates with commitment. Prefer clean drop entries in BJJ, chain when stuffed, and pass on landing.
