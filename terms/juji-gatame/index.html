What is Juji Gatame?

Juji-gatame (ude-hishigi-juji-gatame) is the cross armlock: tori controls uke’s arm between the thighs and hyperextends the elbow until tap or ippon.

Athletes search for angles, escapes, and how judo’s clock differs from long BJJ bottom games.

Kodokan name on Martial Index; armbar.html stays BJJ.

Live siblings via judo hub and Ne-Waza.

How it works

Knees pinch above the elbow as fulcrum; hips extend; hands control the wrist.

Entries from pins, Tomoe Nage, turtle scrambles.

IJF: elbow kansetsu only.

Control first, extension second.

Judogi vs no-gi

Gi control uses lapels/sleeves to kill frames and feed chokes.

No-gi uses head position, underhooks, figure-fours.

IJF contests are judogi events—label no-gi rounds clearly.

Prefer qualified coaching over video-only learning; Martial Index is a map, not unsupervised curriculum.

In practice

From Yoko Shiho Gatame, uke pushes; tori isolates arm, swings leg over, falls to juji, extends smoothly—uke taps.

Film knee pinch line.

IJF notes & safety

Under IJF 2026–28 rules, osaekomi scores yuko at 5–9s, waza-ari at 10–19s, and ippon at 20s. Yuko is live and does not add up to waza-ari; two waza-ari make ippon.

Safety: solid Ukemi before hard nage-komi; tap early; train under qualified coaches.

Build skill in Uchi-Komi / structured drills, then test in Randori.

Common mistakes

  • ‘Pinning’ with back off the mat.
  • Hunting taps without control.
  • Ignoring 5s/10s/20s gates.
  • Skipping partner communication.
  • Ignoring live sibling links in Related notes.

Training notes

Juji is a control conversation.

Pair with pin platforms.

Track whether clock awareness, kuzushi, and ukemi improve—not only highlight finishes.

Revisit the judo style hub when orienting new training partners.

Pin and submission literacy is scoreboard literacy. Under the 2026–28 IJF cycle, five seconds is already a yuko, ten seconds is waza-ari territory, and twenty seconds ends the contest. Train those gates on purpose in newaza randori.

Escape nights belong on the calendar. A club that only awards top pressure produces athletes who panic when roles reverse. Drill the hold, then drill the exit, then switch.

Cross-training note: BJJ cousins (armbar, kimura, americana, rear naked choke) are labelled separately on Martial Index. Use them for contrast, not as replacements for Kodokan names in judo contexts.

Tap early; release faster. Encyclopedia pages cannot feel your partner’s elbow. Coaches and mat culture do that job.
