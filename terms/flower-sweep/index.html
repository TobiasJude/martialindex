Flower Sweep

The flower sweep is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu closed-guard sweep that uses sleeve and pant (or analogous no-gi) control with a hip swing to reverse the top player—tightly clustered with the pendulum sweep.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Sleeve + pant (gi), swing hips, reverse to mount.
  • Pillar: closed guard. Twin cluster: pendulum; also scissor, hip bump, elevator.
  • High-percentage white-to-black belt staple in gi.
  • No-gi needs underhook/wrist replacements for fabric grips.
  • Chain to armbar/omoplata when they post.

What Is Flower Sweep?

Flower sweep teaching usually emphasizes gripping a sleeve and the opposite pant, then swinging through to dump them past your hip line into mount. Many gyms treat flower and pendulum as cousins or synonyms; Martial Index keeps both pages cross-linked for search intent—same closed-guard pillar. It is a key branch of closed guard.

Across: pendulum, scissor, hip bump, elevator, armbar.

How Flower Sweep Works

Break posture, secure sleeve and pant, open guard, swing the free leg and hips through, and pull them over while you sit up into mount.

Top answers: posture, base, strip pants grip, stand into open-guard passing.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Primary home—pants grip is the flower signature for many coaches.

No-gi. Underhook + ankle/knee control analogs; expect more scramble.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Closed guard flower to mount; if they post, branch to armbar. If pants grip fails, switch to scissor or hip bump.

Types / Variations

Classic gi flower

Sleeve + pant + hip swing to mount.

Flower to armbar

Post defense creates the arm.

Flower–pendulum merge

Gyms that teach one name for both—use both catalog pages.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries: closed guard grip fights, failed scissor. Counters: strip pant grip, posture. Cluster chain mandatory.

Flower vs Scissor Sweep

Factor Flower Scissor
Signature Sleeve/pant swing Shin across + chop
Pillar Closed guard Closed guard
Gi bias Strong Strong but less pant-dependent
Cluster Pendulum twin Pendulum / flower siblings

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Legal in all IBJJF divisions. Gi staple.

Safety

Control the dump so partners are not driven onto a tucked neck. Progressive drilling.

Common mistakes

  • No pant (or analog) control.
  • Flat swing.
  • Not coming up to mount.
  • Ignoring pendulum twin page links.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is the flower sweep?

A BJJ sweep that reverses top/bottom using the flower sweep mechanics described above.

Gi or no-gi?

Both, with different grips. Gi uses sleeves/pants/collar; no-gi uses underhooks, head control, and hooks.

Where does it sit in the catalog?

Under closed guard; across to pendulum, scissor, hip bump.

Good for white belts?

Often yes when drilled slowly with a clear hip path—confirm with your coach for your body type.

What if it fails?

Chain to a sibling sweep or attack (armbar/triangle/kimura) instead of forcing one path.

Flower vs pendulum?

High overlap. See pendulum—cross-linked twins.

Best belt level?

White belt onward in gi; refine forever at higher belts.

The Bottom Line

Flower sweep is gi closed-guard gold—sleeve, pant, swing, mount. Keep pendulum as the twin link and scissor/hip-bump as cluster mates.
