What Is Twio Yeop Chagi (Jumping Side Kick)?

Twio yeop chagi (뛰어 옆차기) is the jumping side kick, the aerial of Yeop Chagi (Side Kick).

Impact is typically heel or knife-edge with toes pulled back, same as the ground kick.

Compare with Twio Dollyo Chagi (Jumping Turning Kick): jumping turning kick rotates onto an arc; jumping side kick stays a sideways line.

In modern WT Kyorugi (Sparring) it is less common than cutters and turning volume, but it remains a breaking and demo signature.

How Twio Yeop Chagi (Jumping Side Kick) Works

Chamber like Yeop Chagi (Side Kick): knee up, hip cocks, supporting side turns toward the target.

Jump from the rear or a skip, keep the kicking knee high, then thrust the heel through.

Retract toward Chamber (Bal Deung) before the floor arrives. A locked-out landing is how ankles fail.

Hands stay in a compact guard. Windmilling for height wrecks the line.

Mechanically, successful twio yeop chagi (jumping side kick) work is usually a chain: stance integrity → timing → impact or control → recovery to Taekwondo Stance with hands alive.

If distance is wrong, even perfect form fails—too far and you kick air; too close and you collide into clinches or jammed chambers you did not plan.

Pair offense with the defensive layer this tool implies: kicks need Arae Makki (Low Block)/Momtong Makki (Middle Block) literacy when drills reverse roles; sparring tools need Hogu and Electronic Scoring (PSS) awareness.

WT / ITF / Rules Notes

Under World Taekwondo (Olympic) competition, twio yeop chagi (jumping side kick) is interpreted through World Taekwondo Rules, Kyorugi (Sparring) scoring zones, and often Electronic Scoring (PSS)—not bare gym aesthetics.

ITF Taekwondo schools may share Korean vocabulary while differing on sine-wave motion, hand techniques, and pattern (tul) emphasis; do not assume WT footage equals ITF syllabus.

Gym culture may drill twio yeop chagi (jumping side kick) daily; contest night may shrink the toolkit based on weight class, PSS sensor thresholds, and coaching strategy.

Confirm the event agreement (WT, ITF, open, recreational) before importing sparring volume into contest intensity.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Breaking: a short run into a jumping side kick through stacked boards.

Sparring rarity: a hop side kick used as a long jam when someone sprints in—closer to Push Kick (Mireo Chagi) tactics than a highlight reel.

Fight picture: twio yeop chagi (jumping side kick) appears when range and timing say so, then yields to the next kick or exit back to Taekwondo Stance.

Gym test: can you apply twio yeop chagi (jumping side kick) after a Feint Kick and still recover guard and balance? If not, connect it to Kick Combination work instead of isolating forever.

Types / Variations

Hop / skip jumping side kick

A skip-step into side kick to steal range. Cousin to Skip Step footwork.

Drill this variation with movement—step in, step out, and reset—so it survives sparring pressure, not only pad stations.

Flying / run-up side kick

Common Mistakes

  • Kicking with the toes or the flat of the foot.
  • Turning the hip too late so the kick becomes a sloppy front kick.
  • Looking at the floor instead of the target.
  • Landing on a straight support knee.
  • Copying movie flying side kicks into {kyorugi} without a plan.
  • Treating twio yeop chagi (jumping side kick) as a highlight instead of a range-and-timing skill.
  • Skipping recovery—landing then posing with hands down.
  • Ignoring sibling tools that set or cover the technique.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

Twio yeop vs twio dollyo?

Twio Yeop Chagi (Jumping Side Kick) is a linear side-line jump; Twio Dollyo Chagi (Jumping Turning Kick) is a jumping turning arc.

Heel or blade?

Both appear. Heel is common for power; blade appears in many traditional cues.

Does WT score a jump extra?

No. Valid Palding (Trunk Scoring) or head impact scores; the jump does not add a bonus by itself.

ITF name?

ITF Taekwondo often uses twimyo yop cha jirugi for flying side piercing kick.

Prerequisite?

A stable standing Yeop Chagi (Side Kick) and a safe Twio Ap Chagi (Jumping Front Kick) landing.

The Bottom Line

Treat twio yeop chagi as a jumping Yeop Chagi (Side Kick), not a movie stunt—range and landing beat height.
