Leg Staple Pass

What Is Leg Staple Pass?

Leg Staple Pass is a headquarters / knee-cut family control that staples one of the bottom player’s legs to the mat with your shin or knee while you clear the other side. In gym language you may also hear staple pass / shin staple. Train it as a named pattern with clear entries and exits—not a vague vibe.

How It Works

From HQ or a cleared foot, pin their shin/thigh to the mat with your shin (staple), kill the far knee, then knee-cut, smash, or float to side control. Control beats speed: establish the grips or frames that make the next decision obvious.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi vs no-gi notes. Pant grip on the stapled leg locks it down; no-gi uses shin bone and hip weight.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

In live training, leg staple pass usually appears as a short window: you recognize the grips/angles, commit the mechanics above, and either finish the position change or chain to a sibling technique when they defend.

Types / Variations

Primary pattern

Standard leg staple pass as described in the hero video and fundamental classes.

Competition variation

Competition variation: tighter grips, earlier chains, less telegraphing.

Related option

Related option: transition into a sibling from the Related notes when the primary finish stalls.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries come from the parent position(s) (Guard Passing). Counters include framing early, removing the key grip, and forcing a different cluster (for example recovering guard or changing the pin). Escapes prioritize creating space before the structure fully sets.

How It Compares

Special Considerations

Rules & sport context

Generally legal in BJJ; check IBJJF Rules and ADCC Rules for crank/reaping/slam nuances when relevant to this pattern (especially neck, spinal, and knee pressure).

Safety

Communicate, tap early, and avoid ballistic force on the neck, shoulders, and knees. This page is reference—not live coaching.

Common mistakes

Rushing without grips; stalling without a next move; confusing leg staple pass with a nearby sibling name.
