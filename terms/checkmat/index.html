Checkmat

Checkmat is a major modern Brazilian jiu-jitsu team publicly associated with Leo Vieira’s leadership and a competitive international network. Place it beside Alliance, Atos, and Nova Uniao as team context—not a recruitment page.

Published 12 Sep 2026 · Updated 12 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Modern team born from late-2000s reshuffles.
  • Competitive identity across belts.
  • Local affiliates differ.
  • History involves prior Alliance/Brasa-era chapters in public reporting.
  • No invented personal lineages here.

What Is Checkmat?

A sport-BJJ organization with HQ visibility (e.g., Long Beach coverage) and global schools.

Known through elite competitors and coaching figures in media.

Part of the post-split competitive ecosystem.

How Checkmat Works
