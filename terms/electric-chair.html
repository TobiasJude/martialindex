Electric Chair

The electric chair is a 10th Planet / Eddie Bravo system attack from lockdown half guard that stretches the trapped hip/leg—and doubles as a powerful sweep—bridging into truck and banana-split games.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Deep lockdown first.
  • Stretch and/or sweep—both are “the move.”
  • Forearm/elbow near the knee line on the far leg.
  • Gi possible; no-gi iconic.
  • Link truck, banana split, half guard.

What Is Electric Chair?

Electric chair starts in lockdown: figure-four on one leg, then you elevate/whip to get under the far leg and stretch while extending the lockdown. Flexible opponents may not tap to the stretch; the sweep still wins position.

Cluster: lockdown, truck, banana split, half guard.

How Electric Chair Works

Secure lockdown. Create space, shoot under the far leg onto your shoulder line, re-grip near the knee, extend the lockdown while collapsing pressure for the stretch—or use the off-balance to sweep them over.

If stretch fails, keep the sweep and climb to truck/back.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Works with pant grips assisting lockdown.

No-Gi. Signature Bravo system tool.

Stretch submissions vs sweeps may be judged differently by refs—prefer clear sweeps if rules are vague.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

From lockdown, Eddie-style, Alex gets under Jordan’s far leg, extends, and either taps Jordan with the electric chair stretch or sweeps to top/truck pathways.

Types / Variations

Stretch finish

When flexibility is limited.

Sweep priority

When they are rubbery.

Truck link

After the lift, insert truck.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries: lockdown half guard, butterfly-half transitions.

Counters: free the trapped foot early; do not let lockdown deepen.

Escapes: extract the hooked foot and post—tap if hip stretch peaks.

Electric Chair vs Banana Split

Factor Electric Chair Banana Split
Start Lockdown Truck (often)
Double duty Stretch + sweep Primarily stretch/gateway
System 10th Planet half guard Truck family
Backup Sweep / truck Calf slicer / twister

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

As a sweep it is generally fine; as a stretch submission, confirm event rules. Follow-ups into twister territory need separate legality checks—Twister / spinal-twist finishes and some truck-family attacks are heavily restricted or illegal under many IBJJF-style rule sets. Verify current IBJJF vs ADCC (or promoter) rules before competing. Train spine torque only under qualified coaches.

Safety

Hip and groin stretches. No bouncing. Communicate with partners.

Common mistakes

  • Shallow lockdown.
  • Only hunting tap vs flexible partners.
  • Losing the sweep when stretch fails.
  • Skipping truck literacy.

Related notes

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is the electric chair?

A lockdown attack that stretches the far hip/leg and/or sweeps.

Who popularized it?

Eddie Bravo / 10th Planet system.

Always a submission?

No—sweep is often the money.

Gi or no-gi?

Both; iconic in no-gi.

Link to truck?

Yes—common next control.

Defense?

Kill lockdown early.

Related stretch?

Banana split from truck.

The Bottom Line

Electric chair is lockdown’s stretch-and-sweep engine: deepen the hook, get under the far leg, finish or reverse. Wire it to truck and banana split.
