High Crotch to Cage

High crotch to cage is the skill of riding a high crotch or deep single into the fence, then finishing with lift, run-the-pipe, or corner turns the wall makes available.

By Tobias Mesevage · Published 12 Sep 2026 · Updated 12 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Head outside, arm deep; the fence replaces the missing circle.
  • Lift or run the pipe—do not just smash sideways into the links.
  • Chain to a double or turn-the-corner when the high crotch stalls.
  • Hand-fight whizzers and crossfaces before you elevate.
  • Legal fence contact only.

A high crotch keeps the head outside with a deep hold on one leg/hip—more upright than a low single. In MMA you drive that position into the fence so they cannot hop out.

How High Crotch to Cage Works

Secure high crotch → drive to fence → keep hips heavy on the wall → lift-and-turn, run the pipe, or convert to a double when the free leg is trapped.

Jackson-style cage details still apply: connect under the butt and lift off the fence before rotating.

Common Mistakes

  • Pushing without connecting hands.
  • Ignoring the free-leg hop.
  • Neck open to guillotines while driving.
  • Treating high crotch to cage as a highlight instead of a position-and-timing skill.
  • Skipping recovery—landing then posing with hands down or hips light.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What does high crotch to cage mean?

Riding a high crotch or deep single into the fence and finishing with lift, pipe, or conversion.

Different from a fence double?

Fence doubles attack both legs/hips more squarely; high crotch keeps a one-leg emphasis until you convert.

Need wrestling experience?

Helpful, but MMA gyms teach cage-specific conversions for strikers too.

Common counter?

Hard whizzer, crossface, and sitting the weight into the fence—clear grips or switch finishes.

The Bottom Line

High crotch to cage keeps a one-leg attack alive when the wall appears: stay deep, lift or pipe, and convert rather than stalling on the links.
