What is Kouchi Gari?

Kouchi gari is the minor inner reap: a smaller inside attack to uke’s foot/heel that scores or loads seoi, tai-otoshi, and larger reaps.

Searchers want mechanics, Kuzushi, combinations, and Judo Rules context.

Combination engine for the seoi hub.

Filed under Nage-Waza on the judo hub.

How it works

Timing on the step is everything—catch the foot as it plants or lifts; hands contribute Kuzushi.

Famous seoi setup: kouchi bends the lead leg, then Ippon Seoi Nage or Seoi Nage (Judo) fires.

Sibling Ouchi Gari; cousin Deashi Harai.

Judogi vs no-gi judo

In the Judogi, sleeve and lapel grips create classic levers.

No-gi keeps hip/leg geometry with underhooks, wrists, head position.

Transfers: timing. Not: illegal grip stalling.

Prefer qualified coaching over video-only learning; Martial Index is a map, not unsupervised curriculum.

What it looks like in practice

Tori taps kouchi as uke steps in; weight buckles; immediate seoi without resetting the sleeve.

Small throw, big career value.

IJF notes & safety

Under IJF 2026–28 rules, osaekomi scores yuko at 5–9s, waza-ari at 10–19s, and ippon at 20s. Yuko is live and does not add up to waza-ari; two waza-ari make ippon.

Safety: solid Ukemi before hard nage-komi; tap early; train under qualified coaches.

Build skill in Uchi-Komi / structured drills, then test in Randori.

Common mistakes

  • Hips too far (no tsukuri).
  • Giving up after one entry.
  • Skipping partner communication.
  • Ignoring live sibling links in Related notes.

Training notes

Write kouchi→seoi on the whiteboard weekly.

Catch steps, don’t invent power.

Track whether clock awareness, kuzushi, and ukemi improve—not only highlight finishes.

Revisit the judo style hub when orienting new training partners.

Competition film study helps: mute the commentary and watch only weight shifts for ten attempts of this throw. Count how often the successful ones begin with a step uke did not want to take. Bring that count into your next uchi-komi block.

Dojo programming tip: pair this page’s throw with one complementary attack and one pin for seven days. Combinations and follow-ups appear faster than isolated “move of the week” culture.

If you cross-train Brazilian jiu-jitsu, keep labels honest. Shared mechanics are real; scoring incentives and gripping rules are not identical. Use Martial Index cousin pages with clear BJJ labels when they exist, and keep this article as the judo line.

Safety reminder again because amplitude sneaks up: ukemi quality sets the ceiling for how hard you can train nage-komi. When landings get sloppy, end the throwing block even if your ego wants more.
