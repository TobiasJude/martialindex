Butterfly Half

Butterfly half (half butterfly) is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu hybrid where you keep one of their legs trapped in half guard while inserting a butterfly hook—elevating for sweeps, wrestling-ups, and transitions into X-guard or dogfight.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Half-guard trap + inside butterfly hook = butterfly half.
  • Elevation is the point—do not play it like a static shield.
  • Feeds X-guard, single-leg X, and underhook dogfights.
  • Works in gi and no-gi; no-gi loves the wrestling-up path.
  • Parents: half + butterfly.

What Is Butterfly Half?

Butterfly half is a hybrid bottom position: one of their legs stays trapped as in half guard, while your free foot inserts as a butterfly hook under their thigh or hip—borrowing elevation from butterfly guard. It is also called half butterfly.

The position exists to create lift and angle when a pure knee shield is too static and full butterfly is too open.

How Butterfly Half Works

The trapped leg limits their pass; the butterfly hook elevates their hips so you can sweep, come up to a wrestling dogfight, or transition into X-guard / single-leg X. Upper-body connection (underhook, overhook, or collar/sleeve) times the elevation. Without the hook active, you are just playing ordinary half.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Sleeve and collar help time elevations; pants grips secure the trapped leg while you insert the hook.

No-gi. Underhooks and head position drive wrestling-ups; expect faster transitions into SLX when they stand.

What Does Butterfly Half Look Like in Practice?

Alex's knee shield is getting folded. Alex inserts a butterfly hook under Jordan's far thigh, elevates, and either sweeps Jordan over or comes up into a dogfight underhook.

Gi. Alex grips a sleeve to off-balance before the lift.

No-gi. Alex wins the underhook first, then elevates into a wrestling finish or SLX.

Types / Variations

Basic half butterfly

One leg trapped, one butterfly hook in—default hybrid.

Elevation sweeps

Lift and roll or turn the corner using the hook as the engine.

To X-guard / SLX

When they post or stand, extend the hook system into X or single-leg X.

Wrestling-up / dogfight

Elevate just enough to come up on the underhook and finish like classic offensive half.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries. From knee shield when you need elevation; from full butterfly when they step over one hook; recovering half with an intentional hook insert.

Bottom threats. Sweeps, dogfight, X/SLX, back takes off failed posts.

Top counters. Kill the butterfly hook early, flatten, free the trapped knee, smash or knee-cut.

Butterfly Half vs Full Butterfly

Factor Butterfly Half Full Butterfly
Hooks One hook + half trap Two butterfly hooks
Pass risk Lower immediate pass risk Higher if hooks die
Elevation Strong on one side Bilateral elevation
Common exit X/SLX or dogfight X-guard, sweeps, standing

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Legal. Sweeps score; wrestling-ups that reverse position score like any sweep/takedown depending on the rules. SLX/X exits follow standard open-guard scoring.

Safety

Control partners on elevation drills—do not dump them on their necks. Tap early to kneebars if hooks become entanglements.

Common mistakes

  • Inserting a dead hook with no elevation intent.
  • Losing the half-guard trap while fishing for the hook.
  • Elevating without upper-body connection—easy to smash.
  • Never transitioning to X/SLX when they stand.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

Is butterfly half the same as half butterfly?

Yes—same hybrid: half trap plus one butterfly hook.

When do I choose butterfly half over knee shield?

When you need elevation and sweeps rather than pure distance framing—or when the shield is dying and you can insert a hook.

Does it work in no-gi?

Very well—especially for wrestling-ups and SLX entries.

How do I pass butterfly half?

Kill the hook, flatten their shoulders, free your trapped knee, then pass. Do not let them elevate freely.

Can beginners use butterfly half?

Yes after they understand basic half and butterfly mechanics. Start with simple elevations, not complex X entries.

Butterfly half vs lockdown?

Butterfly half elevates and sweeps; lockdown immobilizes for electric chair paths. See lockdown.

The Bottom Line

Butterfly half mixes a half-guard trap with one butterfly hook to elevate, sweep, and enter X-guard or dogfight. Gi times with grips; no-gi wrestles up. See half guard and butterfly.
