Underhook Drive

Underhook drive means pummeling to an underhook, then walking or bursting the opponent toward the fence or off their base—parented by Pummel, feeding Fence Clinch and trips.

By Tobias Mesevage · Published 12 Sep 2026 · Updated 12 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Pummel first, drive second.
  • Head position makes the underhook steer.
  • Primary goal is often the fence.
  • Expect whizzer counters.
  • Trips and body locks finish the drive.

What Is Underhook Drive?

The underhook is inside position under their arm; the drive is what you do with it—not a static hug.

Win via Pummel → head on the underhook-side pec/shoulder → short steps or a burst → force Fence Clinch or finish Outside Trip (Clinch)/Body Lock dumps.

How Underhook Drive Works

Without head position and hip closeness, underhooks become handles for their Whizzer and Overhook Defense.

Dirty boxers use the underhook to create angles for Short Hooks in Clinch while still threatening the drive.

Mechanically, successful underhook drive work is a chain: establish grip or angle → remove a base or frame → apply the scoring action → recover posture with hands alive.

If distance or fence angle is wrong, even perfect form fails—too far and you claw air; too close without a pin and you collide into frames, whizzers, or submission threats.

Common Mistakes

  • Underhook with hips far away.
  • Driving without head position.
  • Ignoring the overhook they insert.
  • Gas-out by muscling instead of stepping.
  • Treating underhook drive as a highlight instead of a position-and-timing skill.
  • Skipping recovery—landing then posing with hands down or hips light.
  • Ignoring sibling tools that set or cover the technique.

Study Notes

Coaches often describe underhook drive as “simple to show, hard to own.” The gap is usually not a missing secret—it is missing reps under stress, poor fence awareness, or grips that collapse when strikes enter the round.

When you compare gyms, you will see stylistic accents: some put underhook drive behind heavy wrestling; others behind dirty boxing; submission-heavy rooms may leave GNP light. The encyclopedia definition stays stable even when accents change.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

Underhook vs double underhooks?

Single underhook drives and angles; Double Underhooks are a tighter body-lock threat.

Parent page?

Pummel teaches how you win the underhook.

Best finish?

Fence pin plus Fence Takedown, or a trip if they stay in open space.

Defense?

Whizzer/overhook, frames, and circling—see Overhook Defense.

The Bottom Line

Underhook drive is steering with inside position: pummel clean, head in, walk them to the fence or trip—don't just hug.
