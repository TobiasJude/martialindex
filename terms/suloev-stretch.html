Suloev Stretch

The Suloev stretch is a grappling/MMA hamstring (and knee-stress) stretch from back control when the opponent tripods—pulling a leg toward you while driving hips down—related in feel to banana-split and kneebar pressures.

Published 11 Sep 2026 · Updated 11 Sep 2026 · Plain markdown

Key Takeaways

  • Back control + tripod reaction is the trigger.
  • Two hands on one ankle/heel; hips drive down.
  • Primarily hamstring stretch; knee can load too.
  • Rare but viral in MMA; verify BJJ contest rules.
  • Tap early—muscle tears can be abrupt.

What Is Suloev Stretch?

Named in MMA journalism after Amar Suloev, the stretch appears when you ride the back and they tripod to shake you off, bringing a foot into grabbing range. You haul the leg up while your hips pressure their quad/hip line, maxing hamstring length—and sometimes resembling a messy kneebar.

Links: back control, banana split, kneebar, truck.

How Suloev Stretch Works

Keep seatbelt/hooks integrity. When they tripod, secure one heel with both arms (or a strong hooked grip), pull the foot toward your chest, and drive your hips into their thigh/hip.

Do not abandon back control entirely for a fishing expedition—connection first.

Gi vs No-Gi

Gi. Belt and pant grips can assist the pull.

No-Gi / MMA. Where the finish is most famous.

Contest BJJ may categorize oddly among stretches/leg attacks—verify the packet.

What Does It Look Like in Practice?

Jordan tripods to escape Alex’s back control. Alex hooks the near ankle with both hands, pulls, and drives hips down until Jordan taps to the Suloev stretch.

Types / Variations

Classic tripod-from-back

Primary story.

High-ride opportunistic

When you are already high on the back.

Kneebar-hybrid feels

When isolation starts to resemble a kneebar.

Entries, Counters, and Escapes

Entries: back control vs tripod escapes; sometimes after failed RNC when they stand.

Counters: stay low; do not tripod into the grab; hand-fight the ankle seize.

Escapes: put the foot down and flatten—tap if hamstring peaks.

Suloev Stretch vs Banana Split

Factor Suloev Stretch Banana Split
Start Back control + tripod Truck / lockdown paths
Primary tissue Hamstring stretch Adductors / split
Fame MMA highlight rare 10th Planet truck
Backup RNC / rear mount Calf slicer / twister

Special Considerations

IBJJF, ADCC, and sport context

Treat as an unusual stretch/leg attack: verify current IBJJF/ADCC/event rules before attempting in contest. Do not assume MMA legality equals BJJ bracket legality.

Safety

Hamstring tears can happen suddenly. Micro-pressure only; many rooms prefer recognition drills over hard finishes.

Common mistakes

  • Fishing without back control.
  • Yanking explosively.
  • Ignoring RNC when available.
  • Competing unclear legality.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in forums, and under instructional videos—answered in Martial Index voice.

What is the Suloev stretch?

A hamstring stretch from back control vs a tripod escape.

Named after whom?

Amar Suloev (via MMA journalism naming).

Same as banana split?

Related stretch family; different position story.

Legal in IBJJF?

Verify—do not assume.

Main trigger?

Opponent tripoding while you have the back.

Defense?

Avoid high tripods into the grab; stay compact.

Gi or no-gi?

Both; MMA-famous in no-gi contexts.

The Bottom Line

Suloev stretch taxes the tripod: keep the back, seize the ankle, hips down. Prefer RNC when it is there—and verify rules before hunting stretches in brackets.
