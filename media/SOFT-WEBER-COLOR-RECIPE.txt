Soft Weber COLOR v2 final (winner: v2c-skin)
============================================

Exact ImageMagick command (preview bake; long edge ≤1600, quality 88):

magick INPUT -colorspace sRGB -modulate 103,85,99 -brightness-contrast 7x7 -level 4%,93%,0.99 -fill '#e6d2bc' -colorize 4 \( +clone -resize 50% -attenuate 0.08 +noise Gaussian -colorspace Gray -resize 200% \) -compose softlight -composite -resize '1600x1600>' -quality 88 OUTPUT

Parameters vs v1:
  v1:  -modulate 105,75,100 -brightness-contrast 8x6 -level 4%,92%,0.98 -fill '#e8d5c0' -colorize 6  grain attenuate 0.1
  v2c: -modulate 103,85,99  -brightness-contrast 7x7 -level 4%,93%,0.99 -fill '#e6d2bc' -colorize 4  grain attenuate 0.08

Notes:
  - saturate ~85 (was 75) for more human skin while still lightly desaturated
  - hue 99 = gentle warmth nudge
  - colorize 4 (was 6) + slightly cooler fill '#e6d2bc' keeps gi whites clean
  - contrast 7 + level gamma 0.99 = soft fashion clarity without haze or punch
  - slightly lighter softlight grain
