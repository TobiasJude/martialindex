Tobias Mesevage

Writes Martial Index. Brazilian jiu-jitsu purple belt. Boxing and Muay Thai on the side. Building a biotech company. Interested in martial arts, learning, and philosophy.

On the mats

Tobias started Brazilian jiu-jitsu as a white belt at Renzo Gracie NYC. After a move to Los Angeles he trained at 10th Planet West LA and received his blue belt, then trained at Highland Park Fight Club and received his purple belt. He has won a few Jiu-Jitsu World League (JJWL) events.

He boxed as a kid and trained Muay Thai. In college he played soccer.

Off the mats

He likes learning for its own sake — fight systems, and philosophy — and is building a biotech company alongside this catalog.

Martial Index

Martial Index is a place to learn all things combat: every art, every technique, the people who practice them. It is reference material, not live instruction. Train under a coach.
