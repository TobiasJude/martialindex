Key Takeaways

  • Sambo is a Soviet-era jacket grappling system that scores throws and pins and is unusually comfortable attacking the legs.
  • Sport sambo and combat sambo share a jacket (kurtka) but not the same striking rules.
  • Read it beside judo, catch wrestling, and BJJ — the overlapping vocabulary is real, the sport incentives are not identical.

What Is Sambo?

Sambo (an acronym from the Russian for “self-defense without weapons”) is a grappling art and combat sport assembled in the Soviet Union from judo, folk wrestling, and jacket styles. Athletes wear a kurtka — a short, belted jacket — with shorts and often wrestling shoes.

Two public faces matter. Sport sambo is a throw-and-submission contest with a well-known leg-lock meta. Combat sambo adds punches, kicks, and a wider self-defense syllabus. This hub treats sport sambo as the encyclopedia core and notes combat sambo where the rules diverge.

How Sambo Works

Standing exchanges hunt high-amplitude throws and trips. On the ground, pins score and submissions finish — including ankle locks and other lower-body attacks that many judo and IBJJF-style BJJ rulesets restrict.

The jacket gives sleeve and lapel handles closer to judo than to no-gi wrestling, but the shorts and shoes change hip control and the way entries look. Cross-training with judo and Brazilian jiu-jitsu is common; the shared DNA is real.

Lineage and neighbors

Sambo’s founders studied judo and a wide wrestling map. Today the art sits between Olympic judo, catch-style leg attacks, and modern submission grappling. Combat sambo is one reason sambo names show up on MMA cards.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the mats, in gyms, and when fighters compare styles.

Is sambo just judo with leg locks?

No. They share a jacket and many throws, but sambo’s shorts, shoes, pin rules, and legal lower-body submissions create a different sport. Judo’s gi and IJF incentives are their own game.

Sport sambo or combat sambo first?

Sport sambo is the cleaner encyclopedia entry for throws and submissions. Combat sambo adds striking and is closer to an MMA rules conversation.

Does sambo use guard like BJJ?

Sambo players can end up on the bottom, but sport incentives reward throws, pins, and finishing — not prolonged open-guard battles. BJJ is the deeper published guard catalog on Martial Index.

The Bottom Line

Sambo is jacket grappling with a Soviet syllabus and a modern leg-lock reputation. Start here, then judo for throws in a judogi and BJJ for the live ground vocabulary.
