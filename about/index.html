Tobias Mesevage

Writes Martial Index

I grew up around Hudson, New York, and went to a Waldorf school on an organic farm. I played a lot of sports and spent a lot of time reading.

Cross country was the one I loved. The pain, and the fact that there was no one to blame. Fighting is that, with a person in front of you.

My martial arts story isn’t clean. I started at Renzo Gracie NYC my senior year of college, then took a long break after that summer — work, then the pandemic. It wasn’t until 10th Planet West LA that I learned to truly love it. Then Highland Park Fight Club, where I started competing. I’m a purple belt now. I’m back in New York after several years in LA, training six days a week, and I still compete.

I think a life is made of what you actually do — not what you meant to do, not what you know. Good days are the days you act. Bad days are the days you don’t. Training is the cleanest version of that I’ve found. The mat doesn’t care what you meant.

I write Martial Index. I’m building trybrst.com.
