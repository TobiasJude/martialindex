Blue belt fundamentals

Same live catalog, deeper framing: map the guard family, treat closed guard as a system (not a rest position), and own the RNC as a blood choke with clean back control.

  1. 1 Guard Go deeper: place closed, open, half, spider, and De La Riva in one taxonomy so new techniques stop feeling like isolated tricks.
  2. 2 Closed Guard Go deeper: posture breaks into armbar / triangle / kimura chains and sweeps—not a static shell that invites a pass.
  3. 3 Rear Naked Choke Go deeper: seatbelt, hooks or body triangle, carotid finish—and why a neck crank is not a correct RNC.
